# Audit: HR toolkit documents, recruit to grow (original `hr-toolkit.html`)

Scope: the 13 original documents from "Recruit & Hire" (tools 01–05), "Onboard & Integrate" (08–10) and "Perform & Grow" (11–15), plus the page-level elements that shape them. Law as in force on 2 November 2026.

## Summary

- **The appointment letter didn't meet the Labour Codes.** "Offer / Appointment Letter" was titled "Letter of Appointment" but subject-lined "Offer of Employment". It also lacked most particulars in the format that OSH (Central) Rules, 2026, rule 6 prescribes: LIN, type of employment, skill category, Aadhaar with consent, PF/ESI applicability and maternity benefit. The rebuild separates the offer letter from the appointment letter, and Part A of the appointment letter follows the 16-item format.
- **The medical form invited discrimination.** It asked about "Mental Health Conditions", "Family history of chronic conditions", tobacco use, height, weight and BMI. It ended: "misrepresentation may result in withdrawal of offer or termination". The joining form added "Physically Challenged / % of disability". The rebuild only checks fitness for the role after a conditional offer, asks no diagnostic questions, takes written consent (health data is sensitive data under the SPDI Rules) and gives the employer the outcome only.
- **The engagement survey reproduced Gallup's Q12.** All 12 items map one to one ("In the last 7 days, someone recognised my good work", "I have a close friend at work"). Gallup says the Q12 items "are proprietary". The rebuild uses 12 original items in four areas, with two reverse-worded.
- **Statutory references were out of date.** Examples: "Statutory minimum is 12% of ₹15,000/month" (the EPF wage ceiling has been ₹25,000 since 17 Sep 2026), "Gratuity Nomination (Form F)" (Form III under the SS (Central) Rules, 2026) and "PF … Form 2 / Form 11" (the EPF Scheme 2026 replaced the 1952 scheme). The rebuild avoids hard-coded ceilings and form numbers it can't verify, and cites sources on each page.
- **The forms weren't accessible.** Across the toolkit page there were 504 inputs, 108 selects and 28 textareas, but only 2 `<label for>`. Tool cards opened on `div onclick`, and headings jumped from h2 to h4 (42 h4, 0 h3). The rebuild uses labelled fields, buttons and ordered headings (shared components), with data kept in the browser.
