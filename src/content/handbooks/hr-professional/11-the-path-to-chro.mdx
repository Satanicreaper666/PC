The job of a chief human resources officer (CHRO) is very different from the personnel function of a generation ago. A CHRO is expected to advise the CEO and the board on strategy, organisation design, culture and risk, alongside running HR. SHRM's BASK reflects this in its Leadership & Navigation competency, which covers creating "a compelling vision and mission for HR that aligns with the strategic direction and culture of the organization". The roadmap below works whether you are two years in or twelve.

## How HR careers progress

| Stage | Skills to build next | Signs you are ready to move up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HR executive or coordinator | HR systems, payroll inputs, compliance basics, clear writing | You run your processes without supervision and spot problems early |
| HR generalist or specialist | Employee relations, owning hiring for a team, metrics | Managers come to you for advice, not just for forms |
| Senior HRBP or HR manager | Business acumen, stakeholder management, people analytics | Business leaders invite you into planning, and you change decisions |
| Head of HR or HR director | Workforce planning, organisation design, board reporting, building an HR team | You set the people agenda and the HR budget, and your team runs without you |
| CHRO or VP HR | Company strategy, mergers and restructuring, culture at scale, executive presence | The CEO and board treat you as a business leader who specialises in people |

For pay at each stage, use recent salary surveys or job-portal data for your city and industry; ranges differ widely between sectors and company sizes.

### If you are starting out

- Learn the Indian basics properly: the [Labour Codes](/handbooks/labour-law/the-four-labour-codes/), payroll and [tax deducted at source (TDS) on salary](/handbooks/tds/what-hr-must-know/). Many HR roles in India are judged on these first.
- Build spreadsheet skills to the point where pivot tables and lookups are routine.
- Write well. Clear, polite, short emails and letters set you apart quickly.
- Choose internships and first jobs for responsibility and a good manager, not only the brand name.
- Take the [competency self-assessment](/toolkit/competency-self-assessment/) now, and again after a year.

## Building credibility when you are the only HR person

Many people in Indian startups and small companies are the first and only HR person. Credibility comes from results, not from the title.

## Your 12-month development plan

A development plan works when it is written down, small enough to do alongside your job, and reviewed with someone else.

<Figure title="A year of deliberate development" caption="Read in order. Each point has a concrete output, so you can see progress." wide>
  <Timeline
    items={[
      { when: "Week 1", label: "Assess", note: "Self-assessment, plus your manager's and a peer's view" },
      { when: "Month 1", label: "Pick two competencies", note: "One action each, in writing" },
      { when: "Month 3", label: "First review", note: "With your manager: what changed?" },
      { when: "Month 6", label: "A visible project", note: "Something the business can see" },
      { when: "Month 12", label: "Reassess and reset", accent: true }
    ]}
  />
</Figure>

1. **Assess.** Take the [competency self-assessment](/toolkit/competency-self-assessment/) on the eight PeopleCraft competencies, and ask your manager and a peer to rate you too.
2. **Choose two competencies** that matter most for your next role.
3. **Pick one action a month for each**: a stretch project, a course, shadowing a colleague, or reading followed by a short note on what you will do differently.
4. **Review at 3, 6 and 12 months** with your manager or a mentor, and reset the plan.

If you lead HR, run the [HR maturity self-assessment](/toolkit/hr-maturity-self-assessment/) for your function as well. Your own growth and your team's capability are linked.
