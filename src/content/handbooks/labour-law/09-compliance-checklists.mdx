| When | What | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before the 7th | Pay wages for the previous month | Code on Wages s.17 |
| By the 7th | Deposit TDS deducted from salaries (30 April for March) | Income-tax Rules, 2026; see the [TDS handbook](/handbooks/tds/key-dates-and-penalties/) |
| By the 15th | Pay EPF contributions and file the ECR | EPF Scheme, 2026 |
| By the 15th | Pay ESI contributions | ESI rules |
| State due date | Deduct and pay professional tax; pay labour welfare fund where due (often June and December) | State laws |
| Every pay run | Give wage slips; keep deductions within 50% of wages | Code on Wages s.18, s.50 |

## Deadlines that start with an event

| Event | Deadline | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A new establishment starts | Apply for OSH Code registration within 60 days | OSH Code s.3 |
| Someone joins | Appointment letter on joining; enrol in PF and ESI if eligible | OSH Code s.6; Social Security Code |
| A woman joins | Tell her about maternity benefits in writing and electronically | Social Security Code s.67 |
| Service conditions change | At least 21 days' notice to affected workers before the change | IR Code s.40 |
| Someone leaves | Wages within two working days; gratuity within 30 days of becoming payable | Code on Wages s.17(2); Social Security Code s.56 |
| A worker is retrenched | One month's notice or pay; notice to the government; re-skilling fund within 10 days | IR Code s.70, s.83; central rules |
| An undertaking closes (50+ workers) | 60 days' notice to the government | IR Code s.74 |
| A POSH complaint is made | Inquiry completed within 90 days | POSH Act |

Also once a year:

- **Health check-ups:** free annual check-ups for workers aged 40 and above (45 under some state rules).
- **Annual returns under the Codes:** file on the dates your state's or the central rules set. Several state Social Security rules ask for the annual return within 30 days of 31 March.
- **POSH:** the Internal Committee's annual report to the employer and the District Officer, and the case numbers in the employer's annual report. Review the committee's membership; its term is at most three years.
- **Leave:** encash unused annual leave if a worker asks at the end of the calendar year.

## Registers and records

| Record | Required by |
| --- | --- |
| Register of employees, muster roll and wage register | Code on Wages s.50 |
| Notice showing the Code's abstract, wage rates, wage period and pay day | Code on Wages s.50 |
| Wage slips for every employee | Code on Wages s.50; OSH Code s.33 |
| Registers, notices and returns under the OSH Code, including the leave register | OSH Code s.33 and rules |
| Appointment letters for every employee | OSH Code s.6 |
| PF and ESI challans, returns and enrolment records | Social Security Code and schemes |
| Standing orders (300 or more workers) | IR Code s.28 |
| Grievance redressal committee records (20 or more workers) | IR Code s.4 |
| Internal Committee orders, case files and annual reports | POSH Act |
| Contractor licences, contract worker records and proof of wage payment | OSH Code and rules |

The central and state rules prescribe the formats. Keep records where you can produce them quickly: the Inspector-cum-Facilitator can examine them, and incomplete records can be fined on their own.

## The Labour Code gap checklist

Work through these once, assign an owner and date to each, and repeat when your state notifies new rules. The interactive [HR compliance audit checklist](/toolkit/hr-compliance-audit-checklist/) saves your progress.

### Pay

1. Run the 50% wages test on every salary structure and update the base for PF, ESI, gratuity, overtime and leave encashment.
2. Check minimum wages by state, skill category and zone, including for contract workers.
3. Pay monthly wages before the 7th and keep deductions within 50% of wages.
4. Pay exit wages within two working days and gratuity within 30 days.
5. Enrol everyone earning up to <Fact id="epf.wageCeiling" /> in EPF, and in the pension scheme where eligible.
6. Recheck ESI coverage at the start of each contribution period, and watch for new ESI regulations after 20 November 2026.
7. Provide for gratuity, including for fixed-term staff after one year.
8. Pay statutory bonus to employees earning up to <Fact id="bonus.wageCeiling" /> within eight months of the year end.

### People and contracts

9. Give every employee an appointment letter, including staff who joined before 21 November 2025.
10. Give fixed-term staff the same terms as comparable permanent staff.
11. Apply the core-activity test to every contract, and check contractor licences and wage payments at 50 or more contract workers.
12. Adopt standing orders, or the Model Standing Orders, 2026, at 300 or more workers.
13. Classify gig workers, consultants and contract staff by how they actually work.
14. Track inter-state migrant workers and pay the yearly journey allowance where due.

### Workplace

15. Keep hours within 8 a day and 48 a week, with a 30-minute break after at most 5 hours, overtime by consent at twice the rate and within the quarterly cap.
16. Apply the annual leave rules: 180 days to qualify, a day for every 20 worked, up to 30 days carried forward.
17. Provide maternity benefits, nursing breaks and a crèche or agreed allowance at 50 or more employees.
18. Get written consent and put the required safeguards in place before women work at night.
19. Arrange free annual health check-ups for workers aged 40 and above (or your state's age).
20. Set up a grievance redressal committee at 20 or more workers, and a works committee where required.
21. Constitute the POSH Internal Committee at 10 or more employees, train people and file the annual report.

### Records and tracking

22. Keep registers, wage slips and notices up to date, register under the OSH Code, track state notifications monthly and map the employee data you hold before the DPDP duties apply in May 2027.
