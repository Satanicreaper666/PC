## Who must comply

The Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (the POSH Act) has been in force since 9 December 2013. It is separate from the Labour Codes and applies to every workplace, public or private, organised or unorganised. It protects women whatever their role or employment status.

- **10 or more employees:** the employer must set up an Internal Committee (IC). Employers with several locations should make sure every location can reach one easily.
- **Fewer than 10 employees, or a complaint against the employer:** the complaint goes to the Local Committee set up by the District Officer.

The Act gives its remedy to women. Many employers also extend their policy and process to complaints by people of any gender; that is a policy choice, and a good one.

## Setting up the Internal Committee

| Role | Who | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Presiding Officer | A woman employed at a senior level | If there isn't one, the Act allows a nomination from elsewhere |
| Internal members | At least two employees | Preferably committed to the cause of women, or with social work experience or legal knowledge |
| External member | One person from an NGO or association committed to the cause of women, or someone familiar with sexual harassment issues | Brings an independent view to every inquiry |
| Women members | At least half of the members | Check this again whenever a member leaves |
| Term | Up to three years | Plan reconstitution before the term ends |

Use the [IC constitution checklist](/toolkit/posh-ic-constitution-checklist/) to set it up and record it, and display the committee's names and contacts where everyone can see them.

- **Help with the complaint:** if the woman can't write it, committee members should help her put it in writing.
- **Interim relief:** during the inquiry the committee can recommend relief, such as transferring either person, leave of up to three months for the woman, or stopping the respondent from reporting on her work.
- **A fair inquiry:** both sides are heard, see the evidence against them and can respond. Keep minutes and reasons.
- **Appeal:** either side can appeal against the recommendations within 90 days.
- **False complaints:** action is possible only for a complaint shown to be false or malicious. A complaint that couldn't be proved is not, on that ground alone, false.
- **Confidentiality:** keep the identities of the woman, the respondent and the witnesses, and the details of the inquiry, confidential.

Women can also file complaints online through the Ministry of Women and Child Development's SHe-Box portal. Use the [POSH complaint form](/toolkit/posh-complaint-form/) for complaints made directly to your committee. Sexual harassment can also be a criminal offence; if the woman wants to file a police complaint, the employer should help her do so.

- Provide a safe working environment, and treat sexual harassment as misconduct under your service rules.
- Display the penal consequences of sexual harassment and the order constituting the committee.
- Hold workshops and awareness programmes regularly, and orientation for committee members.
- Give the committee what it needs to work, and make sure the respondent and witnesses attend.
- Monitor that the committee reports on time.
- The committee prepares an annual report for the employer and the District Officer, and the employer includes the number of cases and their outcome in its own annual report.

<Callout type="note" title="Where scrutiny is heading">
In June 2026 the National Commission for Women recommended annual POSH audits for establishments with 10 or more employees, and Maharashtra set up a checklist-based POSH inspection framework in May 2026. Expect inspectors to ask for committee records, training records and annual reports.
</Callout>

## Penalties

An employer who doesn't set up an Internal Committee, doesn't act on its recommendations or otherwise breaks the Act can be fined up to ₹50,000. For a repeat, the penalty can be up to twice that, and the business's licence or registration can be cancelled. The penalty matters less than the harm to people and trust when complaints are badly handled.
