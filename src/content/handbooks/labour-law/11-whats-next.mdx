## What is settled

- All four Labour Codes, in force since 21 November 2025.
- The central rules under all four Codes and the Model Standing Orders, 2026, notified on 8–9 May 2026.
- The EPF, pension and EDLI schemes of 2026, in force since 29 June 2026, and the EPF wage ceiling of ₹25,000 a month from 17 September 2026.
- The bonus ceilings of ₹21,000 and ₹7,000 notified by the Centre in August 2026, effective from 21 November 2025.
- Salary TDS under the Income-tax Act, 2025, from 1 April 2026. See the [TDS handbook](/handbooks/tds/what-hr-must-know/).

## The watch list

| Item | Position at 2 November 2026 | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| State rules | About ten states had final rules under one or more Codes by late September; the Centre's target was 31 October 2026 | Check your state's gazette every month |
| ESI regulations and scheme | Old ones saved until 20 November 2026; draft replacements reported in August | Confirm the rules before November payroll |
| National floor wage | Not notified as of mid-2026 | Keep following state minimum wage notifications |
| Gig and platform workers | Registration rules in force; welfare schemes and the aggregator contribution start date still awaited in the latest updates we found | Map who works for you through platforms, and how |
| Worker re-skilling fund | Central fund set up in May 2026; working details awaited | Budget 15 days' wages for every retrenchment |
| What counts as an "industry" | A long-pending nine-judge Supreme Court reference on the meaning of "industry" | Watch the outcome if you run a hospital, school, charity or similar body |
| State Shops Acts | Karnataka's 2026 amendment is read differently by commentators; Maharashtra's 2025 Bill status unclear | Take advice before dropping any state rule |
| Data protection | Main DPDP duties from about May 2027; a proposal to shorten the timeline had not been notified by late October 2026 | Map employee data, consents and retention now |
| Paternity leave | The Supreme Court urged a law in March 2026; none enacted | Decide your own policy |
