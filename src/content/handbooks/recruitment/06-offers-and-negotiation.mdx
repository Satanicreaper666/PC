You found the right person. The offer stage is where many hires are lost, usually to slow decisions or pay that doesn't match the role. Close quickly, fairly and in writing.

## Move from decision to offer in days

Good candidates often have other options, and every day you wait gives them a reason to take one. Set targets for each step and pre-approve pay bands so nobody waits for a signature.

<Callout type="tip" title="Make the call yourself">
The hiring manager should make the verbal offer by phone: say why you chose them, what the role will let them do and the pay. Follow up in writing the same week. A written offer that arrives without a conversation feels like a form.
</Callout>

## Build the CTC around the wage rule

Every Indian offer should show the cost to company (CTC) broken into its parts. Since 21 November 2025, the Labour Codes use one definition of “wages” for provident fund (PF), gratuity and most other dues, and it affects how you split CTC.

<Callout type="law" title="The 50% rule in the Code on Wages">
“Wages” means all remuneration, including basic pay, dearness allowance (DA) and any allowance that isn't on the list of exclusions. Excluded items include house rent allowance (HRA), conveyance, employer PF contributions, statutory bonus, overtime and commission. If the excluded items add up to more than half of total remuneration, the amount above half is added back to wages. Source: Code on Wages, 2019, section 2(y).
</Callout>

Three more checks before the numbers go in the letter:

- **Minimum wage.** Minimum wage protection now covers all employees, so check the wages meet the rate that applies to the role and state.
- **In-hand pay.** Candidates care about what reaches their bank account. Under the Income-tax Act, 2025, in force from 1 April 2026, the new tax regime is the default, and employees can still choose the old regime. Show an estimate with the [take-home salary calculator](/toolkit/take-home-salary-calculator/).
- **Internal fairness.** Compare the offer with people already doing similar work. The [salary band designer](/toolkit/salary-band-designer/) and [pay equity analyser](/toolkit/pay-equity-analyzer/) help. The detail on structuring CTC is in [wages and pay](/handbooks/labour-law/wages-and-pay/) and [CTC design](/handbooks/tds/ctc-design-and-perquisites/).

## Negotiate on the band, not the last salary

Decide before the offer call what can move and what can't. Then negotiate once, with reasons, rather than in rounds.

What to say in the common situations:

- **“I have another offer at ₹18 lakh. Can you match it?”** “Congratulations. Our range for this role is ₹15 to 17 lakh, and you'd be at the top of it. Beyond pay, here's what this role offers: [scope, team, learning, flexibility]. If the other role is the right fit, I'll understand.”
- **“I need a 50% hike on my current CTC.”** “We pay for the role, not as a percentage of previous pay. The range for this role is ₹X to ₹Y, and based on what you'd bring we've offered ₹Z. Here's why.”
- **“Can I have two weeks to decide?”** “I want you to make the right decision. Let's talk again in three or four days about any questions. The offer is open for seven days so neither of us is left waiting.”

<Callout type="law" title="Equal pay applies to offers">
The Code on Wages requires equal pay for the same or similar work regardless of gender. Setting pay as a percentage hike on someone's last salary carries past pay gaps into your company. Anchor every offer to the role's band.
</Callout>

If you offer a joining bonus with a clawback (repayable if the person leaves within a set period), state the amount, the period and how repayment works in the offer letter itself.

## Decide whether to buy out a notice period

Notice periods are set by the candidate's contract with their current employer. Buying out part of it gets them to you sooner, at a cost.

Ask one question: is having this person 60 days earlier worth ₹4 lakh to the business? For a role where each week of vacancy costs revenue, it may be. If you agree to reimburse a buyout, put it in the offer letter, pay it against proof such as the relieving letter or full and final statement, and ask payroll how to treat it.

## Make the offer clear and conditional

### What goes in the offer letter

- Designation, reporting manager, work location and work mode.
- Type of employment: permanent, or fixed-term with the end date.
- CTC broken into fixed pay, variable pay (and how it is earned), employer contributions and any one-time payments.
- Proposed joining date.
- Probation period, how confirmation works, and the notice period during and after probation.
- Conditions: background verification, documents to submit, and a fitness check only if the job genuinely needs one.
- Confidentiality and intellectual property terms.
- How to accept, and the date the offer lapses (seven to ten days is usual).

<Callout type="warning" title="Non-compete clauses">
Restrictions that apply after someone leaves, such as a ban on joining a competitor, are generally void in India under section 27 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872. Restrictions during employment, including confidentiality and a notice period, are more likely to be enforced. Take legal advice before relying on any post-exit restriction.
</Callout>

<Callout type="law" title="Data rules for background checks">
- Under the Information Technology (IT) Act's 2011 rules on sensitive personal data, you need written consent before collecting items such as financial or health information.
- From May 2027, under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may use a vendor to process data only under a valid contract, and you must erase data once its purpose is served unless a law requires you to keep it.
- The Act allows processing “for the purposes of employment” without consent, but whether that covers candidates who haven't joined is unclear. Notice and written consent remain the safe basis for checks.
</Callout>

Keep checks proportionate: identity, education and past employment for most roles; criminal record or credit checks only where the role genuinely justifies them. Don't contact a current employer without the candidate's permission.

### Withdraw an offer only for stated reasons

A candidate who accepts your offer may resign from their job the same day. Withdraw an accepted offer only for a reason the offer named, such as a failed check or false information, tell the candidate in writing and give them a chance to respond first.

## Issue the appointment letter on joining

An offer letter and an appointment letter do different jobs, and only the appointment letter is required by the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions (OSH) Code, 2020. Many employers combine them, which is fine if the final letter carries everything the prescribed format requires.

<Callout type="law" title="Appointment letters under the OSH Code">
Section 6(1)(f) of the OSH Code, 2020 requires every employer to issue a letter of appointment to every employee on appointment, with the information and in the form the appropriate government prescribes. The OSH (Central) Rules, 2026, notified on 8 May 2026, set a format for establishments under the central government. It covers, among other items, designation, type of employment and skill category, date of joining, wages and allowances, Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) and Employees' State Insurance (ESI) applicability, broad duties, maternity benefits for women employees, and Aadhaar only with the employee's consent. Most private employers fall under their state's rules, which states were still notifying in late 2026, so check the format your state prescribes.
</Callout>
