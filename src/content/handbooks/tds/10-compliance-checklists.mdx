## How to use these checklists

These lists cover tax deducted at source (TDS) on salary. Copy each list into your payroll calendar, put a name against every line, and set reminders three working days before each deadline. Review the lists once a year, because forms and values change. The deadlines come from rules 215, 218 and 219 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026; internal dates such as "by the 5th" are suggestions to give you a buffer.

## Every month

- [ ] Update payroll inputs: joiners, leavers, increments, bonuses, arrears and perquisites.
- [ ] Recompute each employee's tax for the year under the regime they declared (new regime if they didn't).
- [ ] Apply the section 156 rebate and marginal relief to residents where they apply.
- [ ] Value perquisites at the rule 15 amounts: car, accommodation, meals, gifts, loans.
- [ ] Process any Form 122 or Form 124 received this month, and recompute.
- [ ] Check new joiners' permanent account numbers (PANs) against their names.
- [ ] Send TDS totals by employee to Finance by the 3rd.
- [ ] Deposit TDS by the 5th; the legal due date is the 7th (30 April for March).
- [ ] Reconcile challan amounts with payroll TDS.
- [ ] Answer employees' TDS questions and note any that repeat.

## Every quarter

- [ ] Give Finance the salary and TDS data for Form 138, by employee and month.
- [ ] Recheck PANs, especially for joiners and anyone whose name changed.
- [ ] File Form 138 by 31 July, 31 October, 31 January or 31 May.
- [ ] Check TRACES (the department's TDS portal) once the statement is processed and clear defaults (see [chapter 9](/handbooks/tds/mistakes-and-audit-triggers/)).
- [ ] Confirm every leaver's final-settlement TDS was recomputed on actual earnings.
- [ ] Run the self-audit: test ten employees' calculations by hand.

- [ ] Announce the proof deadline in writing, with the list of acceptable proofs ([chapter 4](/handbooks/tds/investment-declarations/)).
- [ ] Check each proof; tell employees exactly what is missing.
- [ ] Recompute TDS on accepted claims only, and spread any shortfall over the months left.
- [ ] Run the March payroll as the final true-up for the year.
- [ ] Deposit March TDS by 30 April.
- [ ] Prepare the annual salary details for the last quarter's Form 138, then file it early.
- [ ] Download Form 130 from TRACES for every employee, including leavers.
- [ ] Check names, PANs, TDS and the Part C computation against payroll.
- [ ] Issue Form 130, and Form 123 where salary was above ₹1,50,000, by 15 June.
- [ ] Handle mismatch queries from employees through correction statements.

## When someone joins

- [ ] Record the regime they intend to use for the year.
- [ ] Ask for Form 122 if they had another employer earlier in the tax year, and explain why it helps them.
- [ ] Collect and check their PAN.
- [ ] Collect Form 124 claims if they are on the old regime.
- [ ] Include any previous salary and TDS in their first TDS calculation.

## When someone leaves

- [ ] Recompute tax on actual earnings for the year with you, including every final-settlement item.
- [ ] Deduct any shortfall from the settlement, and tell the employee before it is paid.
- [ ] If more was deducted than the year's tax with you, deduct nothing more and explain how they get the excess back.
- [ ] Note their personal email address so you can send Form 130 after they leave.
- [ ] Remind them to keep their PAN updated with the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) and to transfer their provident fund (PF) rather than withdraw it early.

## One-time checks for the new law

If your payroll hasn't been reviewed since the Income-tax Act, 2025 came into force on 1 April 2026, do this once:

- [ ] Confirm your software uses section 392, the section 202 slabs and "tax year".
- [ ] Switch templates to Forms 122, 123, 124, 130 and 138.
- [ ] Update perquisite values to rule 15 and the house rent allowance (HRA) city list to rule 279.
- [ ] Update offer letters, payslips and policies that mention section 192, Form 16 or Form 12BB.
- [ ] Brief the team on what changed, using the table in [chapter 1](/handbooks/tds/what-hr-must-know/).
- [ ] Test a sample of payslips and a sample Form 130 before relying on the system.
