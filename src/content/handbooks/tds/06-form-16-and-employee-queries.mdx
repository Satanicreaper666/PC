## What Form 130 is

Form 130 is the employer's certificate of tax deducted at source (TDS) from an employee's salary. It replaced [Form 16](/glossary/#form-16) under the Income-tax Rules, 2026, for salary paid from tax year 2026-27. Employers issued Form 16 for the year ended 31 March 2026 under the old rules, and the first Form 130s are due by 15 June 2027.

For employees whose salary for the year is more than ₹1,50,000, also give the statement of perquisites on Form 123. For lower salaries, the perquisite details go into Form 130 itself.

## How Form 130 gets made

TRACES, the income-tax department's TDS portal, builds Form 130 from the quarterly statements you file on Form 138, once the Centralised Processing Cell (CPC-TDS) has processed them. So the certificate is only as good, and as early, as those statements.

### Check before you send

- The employee's name and PAN match your records.
- Part B's tax deducted matches what payroll deducted and Finance deposited for that employee.
- Part C's computation matches payroll: regime, exemptions, deductions, the section 157 relief for arrears if claimed, and any previous employer's salary from Form 122.
- Leavers are included, with the correct period of employment.
- Digitally signed certificates carry a control number, and you keep a log of what was issued.

<Callout type="tip">
File the last quarter's Form 138 by mid-May rather than on 31 May. Processing takes time, and rejections for wrong PANs or unmatched challans need to be fixed before TRACES can produce the certificates.
</Callout>

## When the numbers don't match

Employees can see the TDS credited against their PAN in their annual tax statement (Form 26AS) and the Annual Information Statement (AIS) on the income-tax e-filing portal. Most queries start with "my Form 130 and my statement don't agree".

## The questions employees ask most

| Question | What to tell them |
| --- | --- |
| Why did my TDS go up this month? | Payroll recalculates the whole year every month. A raise, bonus or arrears, proofs that weren't submitted, or a previous employer's salary from Form 122 will all raise the remaining months' TDS. |
| Which regime is better for me? | If your taxable salary is up to about ₹12.75 lakh and you have no other income, the new regime costs nothing. Above that, compare both using your own deductions; [chapter 2](/handbooks/tds/salary-tds/) shows where the old regime starts to win. |
| Can I change my regime in the middle of the year? | Usually not for TDS: most payroll policies fix the regime once you declare it, so check ours. You make the final choice in your tax return, and that only changes your refund or the tax you pay at filing. |
| Why is the TDS in Form 130 different from my payslips? | Check the March payslip, arrears paid after March, and whether you are adding a previous employer's TDS, which is in that employer's Form 130, not ours. |
| I have a home loan. Will it reduce my TDS? | In the old regime: yes, interest on a self-occupied home (up to ₹2 lakh) and principal within the ₹1.5 lakh investment deduction. In the new regime: no. |
| My TDS is zero. Is that right? | Yes, if you are a resident on the new regime and your taxable income is ₹12 lakh or less: the section 156 rebate cancels the tax. |
| I also earn from freelancing. What should I do? | Give us the income on Form 122 and we will include it, so more is deducted from salary; or pay advance tax yourself on that income. |
| Can I claim house rent allowance (HRA) in the new regime? | No. HRA is fully taxable in the new regime, so you don't need to submit rent receipts. |
| I resigned. Will I get the extra TDS back from the company? | Tax already deposited can't be paid back by the company. It counts towards your tax for the year, and any excess comes back as a refund when you file your return. |
| My tax statement doesn't show all my TDS. | Statements are updated after each quarter's return is processed; the last quarter's is due by 31 May. If it is still missing after that, tell payroll so we can check the PAN and challan. |

<Callout type="note">
Keep a log of TDS questions by type. If the same question comes up from many employees, the cause is usually a payroll setting or a communication gap, not the employees.
</Callout>
