## The calendar for tax year 2026-27

Every salary TDS deadline comes from rules 215, 218 and 219 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026. They are the same dates employers used under the old law, with new form numbers.

| What | Due | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deposit TDS deducted in April to February | Within 7 days from the end of the month (by the 7th) | Rule 218(2) |
| Deposit TDS deducted in March | 30 April | Rule 218(2) |
| Government office paying without a challan | The same day | Rule 218(1) |
| Quarterly deposit, only with the Assessing Officer's approval | 7 July, 7 October, 7 January and 30 April | Rule 218(4) |
| Form 138 (formerly 24Q) | 31 July, 31 October, 31 January, 31 May | Rule 219 |
| Form 130 (formerly Form 16) to employees | 15 June after the tax year | Rule 215 |
| Correction statements | Within two years from the end of the tax year in which the statement was due | Form 138 FAQ |

Most salaried employees must file their own returns by 31 July. Form 130 by 15 June gives them six weeks; a late certificate eats into that time.

## What it costs to miss a date

  items={[

    { value: "1.5%", label: "a month on tax deducted but not deposited", note: "From the date of deduction to the date of payment" },
    { value: "₹200", label: "a day for a late Form 138", note: "Capped at the TDS amount; paid before filing" },

  ]}
/>

| Default | Consequence | Law |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tax not deducted, or deducted short | You are treated as an "assessee in default" for that tax, and pay interest at 1% a month or part of a month | Section 398 |
| Tax deducted but deposited late | Interest at 1.5% a month or part of a month, from the date of deduction to the date of payment | Section 398 |
| Tax deducted but never deposited | The amount, with interest, becomes a charge on all the deductor's assets; prosecution is possible | Sections 398(4) and 476 |
| Tax not deducted, wholly or partly | A penalty of up to the tax not deducted | Penalty provisions of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (check the section) |
| Form 138 filed late | ₹200 for every day of delay, not more than the TDS in the statement | Section 427 |
| Form 138 late or incorrect | Penalty proceedings can follow; the department's FAQ points to sections 461 and 465 | Form 138 FAQ |
| Form 130 issued late | Under the 1961 Act, ₹100 a day while the failure continued, capped at the tax. Check the matching provision of the 2025 Act with your tax adviser | Old section 272A(2)(g) |

<Callout type="law" title="Prosecution for not depositing TDS">
Not paying tax you have deducted to the government is an offence under section 476 of the Income-tax Act, 2025. The Finance Act, 2026 amended the prosecution provisions from 1 April 2026, and the department's Budget FAQ says the maximum punishment for non-payment of TDS is now two years, with a fine as the minimum. The same FAQ describes lighter punishment where smaller amounts are involved and the removal of prosecution for unpaid TDS on perquisites. Read the amended section 476 before relying on the detail.
</Callout>

### How late-deposit interest adds up

Interest runs from the date tax was deducted, not from the due date, and any part of a month counts as a full month. A deposit that is a few days late can therefore cost more than a few days' interest.

Set an internal deadline of the 5th for TDS deposits. It leaves two days for bank or portal problems, and the 7th never becomes the 8th.

## About the original penalty calculator

The earlier version of this handbook had a "late Form 16 penalty" calculator that multiplied employees by days late by ₹100. That followed section 272A(2)(g) of the 1961 Act, where the penalty was also capped at the tax deductible. We couldn't confirm the matching provision in the 2025 Act, so the calculator has been removed. Treat Form 130 as due by 15 June with no grace period.
