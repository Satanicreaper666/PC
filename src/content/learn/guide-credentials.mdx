## Three different kinds of credential

People compare XLRI, CIPD and SHRM as if they were three brands of the same product. They are not. Each gives you a different kind of proof, earned in a different way:

- **XLRI** gives you a postgraduate diploma or an executive certificate from a business school. You earn it through courses, cases, projects and exams. XLRI was founded in Jamshedpur in 1949 as the Xavier Labour Relations Institute, and it is among India's longest-established HR schools.
- **CIPD** (the Chartered Institute of Personnel and Development) gives you a UK-regulated qualification and membership of a chartered professional body. You earn it through written workplace assignments, marked against fixed criteria.
- **SHRM** (the Society for Human Resource Management) gives you a certification, SHRM-CP or SHRM-SCP, earned by passing one exam and kept by recertifying every three years.

So the real question is not "which is best?" but "which kind of proof does my next role need, and which way of learning suits me?" The table puts the three side by side.

## Which fits you?

Start from the work you want to be doing in two or three years, then from the hours you can honestly give each week. For someone already working, the realistic choice is usually between SHRM-CP, a CIPD diploma and one of XLRI's part-time routes. The full-time PGDM (HRM) means stepping away from work for two years.

Two honest checks before you commit:

- **Hours, not ambition, decide the route.** Level 7 is a strong fit for someone moving into strategy, but it needs about {CRED.cipdL7Tqt.display} of study in total. If your weeks are already full, Level 5 or SHRM-CP done well is worth more than Level 7 abandoned.

## SHRM-CP and SHRM-SCP: one exam, taken in person

**What it is.** SHRM is a US-based professional body that also serves members here through SHRM India. Its two certifications test the same blueprint, the SHRM BASK (Body of Applied Skills and Knowledge), 2026 edition: eight behavioural competencies, such as Ethical Practice and Business Acumen, plus HR knowledge across 14 functional areas. SHRM-CP is pitched at operational HR work; SHRM-SCP at strategic work.

**Who it suits.** SHRM-CP suits HR generalists, HR business partners and specialists who want a credential employers recognise, earned in one sitting. It rewards good judgement in everyday HR situations. SHRM-SCP suits people who already set policy and strategy.

**Eligibility.** SHRM-CP has no requirement: no HR title, degree or minimum experience. For SHRM-SCP you need either {CRED.shrmScpYears.display} of strategic-level HR work, at 1,000 or more hours a year, or three years holding SHRM-CP. SHRM can audit applications and ask for a job description.

**How it is assessed.** One computer-based exam at a Prometric test centre. Every question is multiple choice, and {CRED.shrmMix.value[0]}% are situational judgement items: short workplace scenarios where only the single most effective action earns credit. The result is a scaled score from 120 to 200, and {CRED.shrmPassMark.display} is a pass.

**From India.**

- **In person only.** SHRM's 2026 Handbook and fees page list no remote option. Find centres and book at [prometric.com/shrm](https://prometric.com/shrm), early in the window, so you get the date and city you want.
- **Fees are in US dollars,** paid by card; SHRM publishes no rupee price, so your bank's forex charges apply. Moving your appointment costs {CRED.shrmReschedule.display}, depending on how close it is.
- **US law.** {CRED.shrmUsLaw.display}. The 2026 Handbook does not restate this, so confirm it for your window with SHRM India before you skip US-law revision.

## CIPD: Level 5, Level 7 or Experience Assessment

**What it is.** CIPD is the UK's chartered professional body for HR and people development. Its qualifications are regulated in the UK and built on the CIPD Profession Map, a free framework of the knowledge and behaviours people professionals need at each career stage. Passing a diploma also gives you a membership grade.

**Who it suits.** People who learn well by reading, writing and reflecting, and who want their practice judged against professional standards. It carries most weight with UK- and Europe-headquartered employers and their Indian centres, with consultancies, and in roles that cover UK, Irish or Gulf staff.

**Eligibility.** There are no formal entry requirements. The minimum age is 18 for Level 5 and 21 for Level 7, and centres check your English and study skills before you start. Student membership is compulsory while you study.

**How it is assessed.** CIPD writes each assessment brief around a realistic workplace scenario. Your study centre marks your work against the assessment criteria, and CIPD then checks a sample. Every criterion must be met. Level 5 is pass or fail; at Level 7 each assessment is graded Pass, Merit or Distinction. [Module 20](/learn/cipd-assignments-and-capstone/) teaches the writing.

**From India.**

- **No local centre.** CIPD's centre finder listed {CRED.cipdIndiaCentres.display} in India in November 2026. Indian learners enrol with an approved centre licensed to teach fully online. - **Time zones.** India is 4.5 to 5.5 hours ahead of the UK, so UK evening tutorials fall around midnight in India. Ask about recordings, or cohorts timed for Asia and the Middle East.
- **UK law comes with it.** Some UK employment law sits inside the compulsory units, so you will study it whichever options you choose.
- **Fees are in pounds.** CIPD publishes no Indian price.

## XLRI: the full-time PGDM (HRM), PGCHRM and EDPs

**What it is.** XLRI – Xavier School of Management teaches HR at every level, from a two-year residential diploma to short executive programmes. Its HR teaching is known for depth in industrial relations and labour law, and for a full general-management core: economics, finance, marketing, operations and strategy.

**The full-time PGDM (HRM).** It runs at the Jamshedpur campus only; XLRI's Delhi-NCR campus offers HR in certificate and blended forms.

- Two years in six terms and {CRED.xlriPgdmCredits.display}. A summer internship, a 4.5-credit capstone project (on top of those credits) and a short, non-credit village immersion are also required.
- Entry is through the XAT exam, then a group discussion or interview.
- Assessment is continuous: case analyses, class participation, quizzes, mid-term and end-term exams, and projects.
- The fee is {CRED.xlriPgdmFee.display}.

It suits people early in their careers, or those willing to step away from work, who want a full management education with HR at its centre.

**Routes for working professionals.**

## How each is recognised in India

No HR credential is a licence to practise in India, and Indian law requires none of them. What each one signals depends on who is reading your CV.

- **XLRI** is one of the most established names in Indian HR education, and carries particular weight for roles that need industrial relations, labour law and plant HR. An executive certificate carries the XLRI name, but it is not the full-time diploma: describe it accurately on your CV.
- **CIPD** carries most weight with UK- and Europe-headquartered multinationals, their Indian global capability centres, consultancies, and roles that cover UK, Irish or Gulf staff. Chartered membership is a portable professional status backed by a code of ethics and continuing development. CIPD qualifications are professional, not academic: we found no Indian regulator treating them as equivalent to a degree (not confirmed).
- **SHRM** certification is widely known among Indian employers, and it has the lowest barrier to entry: no experience requirement for SHRM-CP. Its content is US-flavoured, so pair it with Indian law from [module 11](/learn/indian-labour-law/).

Whichever you choose, the credential opens a conversation; your work examples close it. Keep a record of the projects you lead while you study.

## Doing more than one

Many people take two credentials over a few years. The overlap is large, so the second costs fewer hours than the first. Two common orders:

- **SHRM-CP first, then CIPD.** One exam gives you an early credential. The knowledge then carries into a CIPD diploma, where your hours go into writing rather than learning content from scratch.
- **An XLRI executive programme first, then SHRM-CP.** You gain Indian law and business grounding, then add a widely known certification.

This course is built to serve both: each module carries SHRM-style judgement questions and a CIPD-style written question. See [how this course works](/learn/how-this-course-works/) for study plans, and the [map](/learn/the-map/) for what you will not need to learn twice.

<Callout type="warning" title="Before you pay anyone">
Check a CIPD centre on CIPD's own centre finder, and an XLRI programme on xlri.ac.in, before you pay a third party. Read SHRM's transfer, reset and refund rules before you apply, and note the deadline in US Eastern time, which is 10.5 hours behind India in December.
</Callout>
