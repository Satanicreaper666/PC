  - "Lead workstreams on client engagements across strategy, operations and organisational transformation projects"
  - "Develop hypotheses, analytical frameworks and structured problem-solving approaches for complex client challenges"
  - "Conduct primary and secondary research: expert interviews, financial analysis, market modelling and benchmarking"
  - "Build client-ready deliverables: PowerPoint decks, Excel models and written strategy memos"
  - "Manage client stakeholder relationships at VP/Director level; facilitate workshops and working sessions"
  - "Coach and mentor junior analysts on the team; ensure quality and deadline adherence across the workstream"
  - "Contribute to business development: proposal development, case studies and sector research"

interview:
  - q: "A consumer goods company has seen margins fall for three years while sales stayed flat. How would you diagnose the problem?"
    look: "Breaks margin into price, mix, input costs and overheads, forms hypotheses, names the data that would test each and decides what to check first."
  - q: "Walk me through the most complex piece of client work you built. Who were the stakeholders, and how did you manage competing priorities?"
    look: "Explains the client's question, their own part, how they kept stakeholders aligned and what changed because of the work."
  - q: "How do you protect quality and the client relationship on a project that is running late?"
    look: "Tells the client early with a recovery plan, protects the most important deliverables, resets scope openly and keeps the team from burning out."

  - "Needs a ready-made framework before they can structure a problem"
