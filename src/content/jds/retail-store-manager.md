function: "Operations and supply chain"
level: Manager

summary: "This role runs a retail store as a business: its sales and costs, its team, its stock and the experience customers have. Success means targets met with low stock loss, a stable, well-trained team and a store that meets every legal requirement."
responsibilities:
  - "Own store P&L: revenue targets, shrinkage management, gross margin and operational cost control"
  - "Lead a team of 15–50 floor staff, visual merchandisers and cashiers; drive hiring, training and retention"
  - "Manage in-store inventory: replenishment planning, stock accuracy, cycle counts and loss prevention"
  - "Deliver customer experience targets: conversion rate, ATV (average transaction value), basket size and NPS"
  - "Execute brand and visual merchandising guidelines in partnership with the national VM team"
  - "Keep the store compliant: fire safety, registration under the state's shops and establishments law, the Labour Codes and legal metrology (weights and measures)"
  - "Analyse daily sales dashboards; take corrective action on underperforming categories or time slots"

mustHave:
  - "Has managed a store, including at least two years as store manager"
  - "Has run a store's P&L and stock without day-to-day supervision"
  - "Has led a frontline team of 15 or more people"
  - "Uses point-of-sale and inventory systems confidently"
goodToHave:
  - "Experience in an organised retail chain"
  - "Experience with omnichannel services such as click-and-collect or in-store returns of online orders"
  - "A retail management qualification"
interview:
  - q: "Your store has missed its sales target for three months in a row. How do you diagnose it, and what do you do?"
    look: "Breaks sales into footfall, conversion and basket size, compares with similar stores, checks stock and staffing, and acts on the biggest gap with a clear review date."
  - q: "How do you keep a frontline team motivated when sales are slow and morale is low?"
    look: "Talks to the team honestly, sets small achievable goals, recognises effort fairly, fixes the irritants they control and looks after rosters and breaks."
  - q: "Describe a major stock discrepancy you handled. How did you investigate it?"
    look: "Follows a fair, documented process, checks systems and processes before blaming people, involves loss prevention and closes the gap that allowed it."
greenFlags:
  - "Knows their store's conversion rate, average transaction value and stock loss, and what moves each"
  - "Has grown sales in a declining category through a local initiative"
  - "Reduced attrition in a frontline team with specific changes"
redFlags:
  - "Focuses only on sales and ignores stock loss and costs"
  - "Describes leading a team as making sure people follow the standard procedure"
  - "Has never investigated a stock discrepancy"
