summary: "This role runs a brand's digital acquisition and engagement across paid media, search, social and lifecycle channels. Success is judged by return on spend and customers won, not by reach alone."
responsibilities:
  - "Own paid media budgets of about ₹50 lakh to ₹5 crore a month across Google, Meta, programmatic and YouTube"
  - "Drive SEO strategy: technical SEO, content strategy, link building and Core Web Vitals optimisation"
  - "Build and manage lifecycle marketing campaigns: email, WhatsApp, push notification and SMS using marketing automation platforms"
  - "Analyse campaign performance using Google Analytics 4, Meta Ads Manager and attribution tools (AppsFlyer, Adjust)"
  - "Develop content strategy for social media (Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube) aligned to brand positioning and SEO goals"
  - "Collaborate with product and design teams on landing page CRO (conversion rate optimisation)"
  - "Report on digital marketing ROI weekly; manage agency and contractor relationships across channels"
competencies:
  - { name: "Google and Meta paid advertising", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "SEO and content strategy", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Marketing automation (Klaviyo/WebEngage/MoEngage)", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Analytics and attribution (GA4, AppsFlyer)", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "CRO and landing page optimisation", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Budget management", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
mustHave:
  - "Has worked in digital marketing with direct ownership of budgets, not only campaign execution"
  - "Has run Google and Meta campaigns at scale; platform certifications help"
  - "Uses GA4 and at least one attribution tool"
  - "Can show a lower acquisition cost or a higher return on ad spend, with numbers"
goodToHave:
  - "Experience with regional-language content, YouTube Shorts or the WhatsApp Business platform"
  - "Performance marketing for D2C, BFSI or healthcare"
  - "Experience briefing and managing agencies"
interview:
  - q: "How would you plan and split a ₹1 crore monthly budget for a new D2C brand?"
    look: "Starts with goals and unit economics, keeps a tested core plus experiments, sets measures and review points, and explains how budget moves on evidence."
  - q: "Your Google Ads return on spend has fallen 30% in two weeks. How do you diagnose it?"
    look: "Checks tracking first, then breaks results down by campaign, search term, device and region, looks at competition and landing pages, and fixes the cause rather than cutting spend blindly."
  - q: "How would you approach SEO for a website in English and three Indian languages?"
    look: "Plans separate, properly tagged language versions with real translated or original content, researches keywords in each language and measures each version on its own."
greenFlags:
  - "Knows their account's return on ad spend, click-through rate and cost per click, and what moved them"
  - "Has run a full-funnel campaign with attribution data"
  - "Ran an experiment that changed how budget was split between channels"
redFlags:
  - "Can't explain the difference between return on ad spend and the true return on marketing"
  - "Has executed campaigns only, with no budget or strategy responsibility"
  - "Hasn't adapted campaigns for regional-language or WhatsApp-first users"
