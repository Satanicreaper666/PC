title: "Senior product manager"
description: "Senior product manager job description for B2B SaaS: own the roadmap, run customer discovery, write PRDs, plan launches and move product measures."
industry: "Technology and SaaS"
function: "Product and design"
level: Senior
experience: "5–8 years"
stage: recruit
checked: 2026-11-02
summary: "This role owns the roadmap for a B2B software product and decides what gets built and why. Success means features that customers use and that move an agreed business measure, such as activation or retention, within two or three quarters."
responsibilities:
  - "Own the product roadmap, tie it to company goals and keep testing it against what customers tell you"
  - "Write clear product requirement documents (PRDs) and plan work with engineering, design, sales and marketing"
  - "Run customer research, both interviews and data analysis, to find unmet needs and turn them into testable ideas"
  - "Own the product's core measures, such as activation, retention and customer satisfaction, and set up tracking with the data team"
  - "Plan launches with marketing and sales: positioning, enablement and how success will be measured"
  - "Keep leaders, customers and partners informed about priorities, trade-offs and progress"
  - "Build privacy into product decisions and collect only the data you need, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (most duties apply from May 2027)"
competencies:
  - { name: "Product strategy and roadmapping", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "User research and synthesis", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "SQL and data analysis", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Agile delivery", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Stakeholder influence", type: Behavioural, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Structured problem-solving", type: Behavioural, level: "Proficient" }
mustHave:
  - "Has owned a B2B software product, or a major part of one, from discovery to launch"
  - "Has shipped features that moved a core measure, and can show the before and after"
  - "Uses product analytics tools such as Mixpanel or Amplitude and can write basic SQL"
  - "Writes PRDs that engineers can build from without constant clarification"
goodToHave:
  - "Experience at a growing startup"
  - "Knows how Indian small businesses or enterprises buy software"
  - "A background in engineering or design"
interview:
  - q: "Tell me about a product decision where customer feedback and the business case pointed in different directions. What did you decide, and why?"
    look: "Names the trade-off, the evidence on each side and who they consulted. A strong answer explains the rule they used to decide and what happened afterwards, including what they would change."
  - q: "Tell me about a feature you shipped that didn't work. What did you learn from the review?"
    look: "Owns the outcome instead of blaming others, explains how they found out it wasn't working (from data, not opinion) and names a concrete change to how they work now."
  - q: "How do you decide what not to build?"
    look: "Describes a repeatable way to prioritise against goals, and gives a real example of saying no and how they explained it to the people affected."
greenFlags:
  - "Explains the reason behind each feature they shipped, with the evidence they used"
  - "Has started customer research on their own initiative"
  - "Has removed or stopped a feature when the data showed it wasn't working"
redFlags:
  - "Can't say how any feature they shipped affected a measurable outcome"
  - "Describes the roadmap as a list handed down by their manager, with no part in shaping it"
  - "Answers stay at the level of buzzwords even when asked for specifics"
