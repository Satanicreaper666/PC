summary: "This role builds the engine that takes a product from early traction to repeatable growth, across acquisition, activation, retention and referral. Success means growth that is measurable, affordable and reported honestly to the board."
responsibilities:
  - "Own acquisition, activation, retention and referral across organic, paid and partnership channels"
  - "Run a steady cycle of growth experiments: hypothesis, test, measure, then scale or stop"
  - "Work with product on onboarding, activation and growth features inside the product"
  - "Set and meet targets for customer acquisition cost, lifetime value, payback period and monthly growth, and report them to the board"
  - "Lead a team of 4–8 people across performance marketing, SEO and lifecycle marketing"
  - "Build channels that suit Indian users, such as WhatsApp, regional-language content, creators and communities"
  - "Report full-funnel numbers every week and keep the growth model up to date"

mustHave:
  - "Has led growth or performance marketing, including managing a team"
  - "Has owned a user or revenue growth number"
  - "Knows at least two acquisition channels in depth, with acquisition-cost results to show"
  - "Understands how Indian users discover, pay and buy, including UPI, regional-language journeys and WhatsApp"
goodToHave:
  - "Has helped a funded startup grow from early traction to scale"
  - "Experience with product-led growth"
  - "Has worked with both consumer and business-to-business-to-consumer models"
interview:
  - q: "Walk me through a growth model you built. What were the key assumptions, and how did actual results compare?"
    look: "Builds from clear inputs (traffic, conversion, retention, cost), explains assumptions with evidence and is candid about where reality differed and what they changed."
  - q: "Tell me about an experiment that failed. What was your hypothesis and what did you learn?"
    look: "States a testable hypothesis and a success measure set in advance, reads the result honestly and shows how the learning changed the next experiment."
  - q: "How would you build growth among users in smaller cities, where habits differ from the metros?"
    look: "Starts with user research, considers language, payment, trust and device limits, and proposes small tests before large spending."
greenFlags:
  - "Can build a growth model from scratch with reasonable assumptions"
  - "Has built a channel from zero rather than inheriting one"
  - "Treats retention as a growth lever, not only an engagement measure"
redFlags:
  - "Equates growth with running digital ad campaigns"
  - "Can't explain contribution margin or payback period"
  - "Has never built or managed a team, for a role that leads one"
