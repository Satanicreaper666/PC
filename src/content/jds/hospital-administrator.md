summary: "This role runs the non-clinical side of a hospital, from the front office and housekeeping to billing, vendors and statutory reports. Success means services that work reliably for patients and staff, accurate and timely billing, and a hospital that is always ready for inspection."
responsibilities:
  - "Oversee day-to-day hospital operations: front office, housekeeping, facilities, security and dietary services"
  - "Manage vendor contracts for medical equipment, linen, waste management and facility maintenance"
  - "Coordinate with clinical department heads on resource planning, bed management and roster issues"
  - "Own billing and revenue cycle management: accurate tariff application, insurance claim submission and collection turnaround time"
  - "Implement and monitor patient feedback mechanisms: exit surveys, complaint resolution and service recovery"
  - "Prepare statutory reports and renewals, such as PCPNDT records, biomedical waste returns, fire safety certificates and municipal licences"
  - "Support NABH compliance documentation, mock drills and audit readiness activities"

mustHave:
  - "A degree in hospital or healthcare administration (MHA, MBA or PGDM), or equivalent experience"
  - "Has used a hospital billing system and handled TPA and insurance claims"
  - "Knows the PCPNDT Act, the biomedical waste rules and the state's healthcare regulations in practice"
  - "Has managed non-clinical teams such as housekeeping, security and dietary staff"
goodToHave:
  - "NABH internal auditor training"
  - "Experience with ISO 9001 or ISO 14001 in a healthcare setting"
  - "Has configured a hospital information system end to end"
interview:
  - q: "A major TPA has delayed cashless approvals for 72 hours during a busy admissions period. What do you do?"
    look: "Protects patients first, escalates through the right contacts at the TPA and insurer, keeps patients informed about their options, tracks every pending case and reviews the process afterwards."
  - q: "How do you handle a department head who keeps ignoring administrative procedures?"
    look: "Talks privately and early, explains the reason for the procedure and the risk of ignoring it, looks for a workable fix and escalates through the right channel if it continues."
  - q: "What are the three biggest compliance risks for a 200-bed multi-speciality hospital, and how would you manage them?"
    look: "Names specific, current risks, such as biomedical waste, fire safety, PCPNDT records or licence renewals, with an owner, checks and an audit routine for each."
greenFlags:
  - "Resolved a TPA dispute and improved claim turnaround, with numbers to show"
  - "Explains the biomedical waste categories and how each must be handled"
  - "Cut patient complaints by fixing a cause, not just by handling each case"
redFlags:
  - "Has no experience running support services, for a role that manages them"
  - "Can't explain how billing codes link to insurance and government scheme tariffs"
  - "Has never prepared for a regulatory inspection or statutory audit"
