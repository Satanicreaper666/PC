title: "Plant head"
description: "Plant head job description: run a manufacturing plant end to end, with safety, quality, delivery, cost, industrial relations and compliance under the OSH Code."
industry: "Manufacturing"
function: "Operations and supply chain"
level: Director
experience: "15–22 years"
stage: recruit
checked: 2026-11-02
summary: "This role runs a manufacturing plant end to end and is accountable for its safety, quality, delivery, cost and people. Success means a safe plant, rising productivity, reliable delivery to customers and constructive relations with the workforce and its union."
responsibilities:
  - "Lead end-to-end manufacturing operations for a plant with 500–2,000 employees; own safety, quality, delivery, cost and morale (SQDCM)"
  - "Deliver the annual operating plan: capacity use, overall equipment effectiveness (OEE), cost of goods, capital spending and workforce productivity"
  - "Own statutory compliance: the OSH Code and the state's rules (which replaced the Factories Act from 21 November 2025), pollution control consents, the other Labour Codes, PF and ESI"
  - "Lead industrial relations under the Industrial Relations Code: regular dialogue with the union, wage settlements and productivity agreements"
  - "Lead capital expenditure projects: new line installation, capacity expansion and automation initiatives"
  - "Partner with supply chain, sales and R&D on demand forecasting, new product introductions and customer-specific requirements"
  - "Develop the plant leadership team: department heads, line managers and frontline supervisors"
competencies:
  - { name: "Manufacturing operations and OEE", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "P&L and budgetary management", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Industrial relations", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Lean manufacturing and TPM", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Safety management (EHS)", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Strategic leadership", type: Behavioural, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Stakeholder management", type: Behavioural, level: "Expert" }
mustHave:
  - "An engineering degree, typically mechanical, electrical, chemical or production"
  - "Has run a plant, or a large part of one, with full accountability for its budget and results"
  - "Has led industrial relations, including union negotiations and wage settlements"
  - "Can show improvements in OEE, productivity or cost, with data"
goodToHave:
  - "An MBA or similar management qualification"
  - "Plant-level recognition for TPM or lean work"
  - "Has introduced automation or Industry 4.0 tools"
interview:
  - q: "Walk me through the hardest operational problem you've faced running a plant. How did you diagnose it, and what did you do?"
    look: "Uses data from the shop floor, separates symptoms from causes, involves the people closest to the work and shows an improvement that lasted."
  - q: "How do you approach industrial relations with a long-standing union that resists changes to productivity?"
    look: "Builds trust through regular dialogue, shares business facts openly, negotiates in good faith within the Industrial Relations Code and links changes to fair gains for workers."
  - q: "Describe the largest capital project you've led. What were the scope, budget and timeline, and what was your part?"
    look: "Gives specific numbers, explains how they managed risk, safety and commissioning, and is honest about overruns and what they learned."
greenFlags:
  - "Has resolved a labour dispute without stopping production"
  - "Can show a sustained improvement in OEE, with the data and the steps behind it"
  - "Has led a capital project from approval to commissioning"
redFlags:
  - "Has managed only a single line or department, for a role that runs a whole plant"
  - "Can't explain the employer's duties under the OSH Code and the state's rules"
  - "Treats serious safety incidents as bad luck rather than finding and fixing the causes"
