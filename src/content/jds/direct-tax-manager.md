title: "Direct tax manager"
description: "Direct tax manager job description: corporate tax returns, assessments and appeals, transfer pricing, TDS and the move to the Income-tax Act, 2025."
industry: "Consulting and professional services"
function: "Finance and risk"
level: Manager
experience: "5–9 years"
stage: recruit
checked: 2026-11-02
summary: "This role manages a company's corporate income tax: returns, assessments, transfer pricing and TDS. Success means accurate, timely filings, well-defended positions in assessments and a smooth move to the Income-tax Act, 2025, which applies from 1 April 2026 while earlier years stay under the 1961 Act."
responsibilities:
  - "Manage corporate income tax compliance: advance tax computation, return filing and self-assessment tax reconciliation"
  - "Handle assessments and appeals, drafting submissions to the assessing officer and the appellate authorities"
  - "Oversee transfer pricing: the study and documentation, the accountant's report, advance pricing agreements and disputes"
  - "Manage TDS compliance across all expense categories; review TRACES reconciliation and TDS return filing"
  - "Provide tax advisory on business transactions: M&A, restructuring, ESOP taxation and cross-border payments"
  - "Review and advise on tax implications of contracts, investments and new business models"
  - "Partner with finance to implement tax positions in ERP and ensure deferred tax accounting (Ind AS 12 / IAS 12)"
  - "Manage the move to the Income-tax Act, 2025: new section and form references from tax year 2026-27, with earlier years still under the 1961 Act"
competencies:
  - { name: "Corporate income tax compliance", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Transfer pricing", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Tax litigation and appeals", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "TDS compliance", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Ind AS and tax accounting", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Tax advisory", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Executive communication", type: Behavioural, level: "Proficient" }
mustHave:
  - "A chartered accountant (CA)"
  - "Has worked in direct tax in industry or at an advisory firm"
  - "Has handled assessments and drafted appeal submissions independently"
  - "Knows transfer pricing documentation and TDS across payment types"
goodToHave:
  - "A law degree as well as the CA qualification"
  - "International tax experience: tax treaties, withholding tax and BEPS"
  - "Experience with advance pricing agreements or mutual agreement procedures"
interview:
  - q: "How would you respond to a scrutiny assessment notice received by the company?"
    look: "Checks the notice, the year and which Act applies, gathers documents, meets every deadline, agrees the approach with advisers where needed and keeps a clear record of submissions."
  - q: "How would you price a management fee charged by an Indian parent company to a foreign subsidiary?"
    look: "Establishes what services are actually provided and their benefit, picks a suitable transfer pricing method with comparables, documents it and considers withholding tax and treaty rules."
  - q: "Describe a tax position you took that was challenged in assessment. How did you defend it?"
    look: "Explains the position and the evidence, how they built the submission, the outcome and what they changed in documentation afterwards."
greenFlags:
  - "Has defended a tax position in assessment and can explain the arguments that worked"
  - "Has prepared transfer pricing documentation that was accepted without adjustment"
  - "Explains the general anti-avoidance rule (GAAR) and how it applies to a real transaction"
redFlags:
  - "Has done compliance filing only, with no assessment or advisory work, for a role that needs both"
  - "Can't explain the difference between the processing of a return and a scrutiny assessment"
  - "Relies entirely on outside advisers for judgement"
