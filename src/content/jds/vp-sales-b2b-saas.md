  - "Write sales playbooks, ideal customer profiles and guides to common objections"
  - "Keep CRM data clean so that pipeline, forecasts and deal speed are visible"
  - "Work with marketing on account-based campaigns, content and lead conversion"
  - "Negotiate enterprise contracts, including commercial terms, legal changes and service levels"
  - "Report the pipeline every week and the revenue forecast every quarter to the CEO and board"

interview:
  - q: "How would you build the sales process for a new product segment from zero?"
    look: "Starts with the customer and how they buy, tests messages on a few deals, defines stages and exit criteria, then hires and scales only once wins repeat."
  - q: "How do you tell whether a missed quarter is a pipeline, conversion or pricing problem?"
    look: "Uses funnel data by stage, segment and salesperson, compares it with past quarters and win-loss notes, and names the fix that follows from each diagnosis."
  - q: "Tell me about the largest enterprise deal you closed. What made it complex, and how did you manage it?"
    look: "Maps the buying group, handles procurement and legal, manages risks to the timeline and is clear about their own part compared with the team's."

  - "Separates pipeline problems from conversion problems"

  - "Can't state the average deal size, sales cycle or win rate in their current role"
