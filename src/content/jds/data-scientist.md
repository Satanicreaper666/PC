title: "Data scientist"
description: "Data scientist job description: build and ship machine-learning models, design A/B tests, build dashboards and explain results in business terms."
industry: "Technology and SaaS"
function: "Engineering and data"
level: Senior
experience: "3–6 years"
stage: recruit
checked: 2026-11-02
summary: "This role turns business questions into analyses and models that product and revenue teams act on. Success means models running in production within weeks, and decisions that leaders can trace back to sound analysis."
responsibilities:
  - "Build, test and deploy machine-learning models for problems such as recommendations, churn, pricing and fraud"
  - "Design and analyse A/B tests, and advise teams on sound experiment design"
  - "Work with data engineering on the quality and speed of feature pipelines"
  - "Build self-serve dashboards and alerts for the measures the business relies on"
  - "Explain model results to leaders in plain language and in terms of business impact"
  - "Contribute to model governance and bias checks, and handle personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (most duties apply from May 2027)"
competencies:
  - { name: "Machine-learning modelling", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Statistics and A/B testing", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Python and SQL", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Feature engineering", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "MLOps", type: Technical, level: "Working knowledge" }
  - { name: "Data storytelling", type: Behavioural, level: "Proficient" }
mustHave:
  - "Has shipped at least two models to production and seen them used"
  - "Writes Python and SQL fluently"
  - "Has sound statistics: hypothesis testing, confidence intervals and regression"
  - "Has used a cloud machine-learning platform such as Vertex AI, SageMaker or Azure ML"
goodToHave:
  - "Published research or strong results in data science competitions"
  - "Natural language processing for Indian languages"
  - "Has worked where models were used inside a live product"
interview:
  - q: "Walk me through a model you built end to end, from framing the problem to production. What happened after it shipped?"
    look: "Starts from the business problem, explains choices about data, features and model with reasons, and describes monitoring and the measured effect after launch."
  - q: "How do you design an A/B test when the sample size is small?"
    look: "Talks about statistical power and the smallest effect worth detecting, options such as longer tests or more sensitive measures, and is honest about when a test isn't worth running."
  - q: "Describe a time your analysis contradicted what leaders expected. How did you handle it?"
    look: "Checked their own work first, presented the finding clearly with its limits, and helped the team decide what to do next rather than simply being proved right."
greenFlags:
  - "Explains a model's impact in rupees or percentage points, not only in accuracy measures"
  - "Can walk through past work in detail; a portfolio helps, but client work is often confidential"
  - "Asks about data quality before asking about computing power"
redFlags:
  - "Has worked only on competition datasets, with nothing in production"
  - "Can't explain why they chose a particular model"
  - "Treats correlation as proof of cause"
