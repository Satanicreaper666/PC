summary: "This role runs the HR processes and systems that every employee relies on, from joining to leaving. Success means accurate data, payroll inputs and statutory filings done on time, and final settlements paid within the legal deadline."
responsibilities:
  - "Run the HRMS: data accuracy, workflows and system upgrades"
  - "Manage lifecycle paperwork, from appointment letters (which the OSH Code requires for every employee) to promotions, transfers and exits"
  - "Own payroll inputs, including variable pay, leave encashment and full and final settlements; wages due on leaving must be paid within two working days under the Code on Wages"
  - "Keep the statutory calendar for PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS, issue Form 130 (which replaced Form 16 from tax year 2026-27) and stay ready for audit"
  - "Support POSH compliance: the Internal Committee, training, records and the annual report"
  - "Maintain employee records, digital filing and document retention as per statutory requirements"
  - "Build and maintain HR dashboards: headcount, attrition, tenure distribution and compliance tracker for leadership reporting"

  - { name: "POSH compliance", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }

  - { name: "Vendor management", type: Behavioural, level: "Proficient" }
mustHave:
  - "Has worked in HR operations, payroll or HR shared services"
  - "Knows Indian payroll law in practice: PF, ESI, professional tax, salary TDS, gratuity and full and final settlement"
  - "Has administered an HRMS such as Keka or Darwinbox"
  - "Knows the POSH Act and has supported an Internal Committee's work"
goodToHave:
  - "Has moved an organisation from one HRMS to another"
  - "Has handled compliance across several states, each with its own professional tax and shops and establishments rules"
  - "Experience in HR shared services"
interview:
  - q: "Walk me through a full and final settlement for an employee who resigned, served three months' notice and has earned leave left."
    look: "Lists every component (pay to the last day, leave encashment, bonus, gratuity if eligible, and deductions or recoveries the contract allows), pays wages within two working days under the Code on Wages and gratuity within 30 days of it becoming payable, and gives the employee a clear statement."
  - q: "How do you keep HRMS data accurate when several people update records?"
    look: "Uses clear ownership, access controls, maker-checker approval for sensitive fields and regular audits, and fixes the source of repeated errors."
  - q: "What does your statutory compliance calendar look like? Name the critical monthly, quarterly and yearly deadlines."
    look: "Covers PF and ESI payments and returns, professional tax by state, TDS deposits and quarterly statements, Form 130 by 15 June, the POSH annual report and state returns, each with an owner and a reminder."
greenFlags:
  - "Can show a clean record in statutory filings and audits, and explain the checks behind it"
  - "Has led an HRMS migration or major upgrade"
  - "Explains PF contributions and the current wage ceiling ({{fact:epf.wageCeiling}}) accurately"
redFlags:
  - "Has outsourced compliance and can't explain the rules behind it"
  - "Can't work out a full and final settlement without the HRMS"
  - "Reference checks show lapses in POSH records or missed annual reports"
