summary: "This role leads sales for a developer's residential projects, managing a sales team and a network of channel partners. Success means booking targets met at healthy prices, buyers treated fairly and every sale documented in line with RERA."
responsibilities:
  - "Own sales targets for a residential project portfolio (volume, value and velocity metrics)"
  - "Manage a team of 8–15 sales executives and tele-calling staff; drive hiring, training and incentive design"
  - "Build and manage the network of channel partners (brokers): selection, commission rules and joint selling"
  - "Convert site visits: manage walk-ins, follow-up cadence, negotiation and documentation through booking to agreement"
  - "Coordinate with CRM teams on lead pipeline, conversion rates and dead lead revival campaigns"
  - "Manage NRI and HNI customer engagement: NRI-focused sales events, FEMA-compliant investment structuring"
  - "Partner with the marketing team on digital lead quality, micro-market positioning and campaign ROI"
  - "Make sure marketing claims, bookings and agreements follow the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 and the state's RERA rules"

mustHave:
  - "Has sold residential property for a developer, including at least two years leading a team"
  - "Can show booking results their team achieved"
  - "Has a working network of channel partners in the relevant market"
  - "Knows the RERA, registration and stamp duty steps, and how home loans work"
goodToHave:
  - "Registered as a real estate agent under RERA, or has completed the training"
  - "Experience with premium or luxury housing"
  - "Experience with plotted developments or townships"
interview:
  - q: "Two competing projects nearby have just cut prices. How do you protect your pricing and keep sales moving?"
    look: "Understands why buyers would choose each project, strengthens the value case, uses targeted offers within approved limits and tracks the effect closely."
  - q: "How would you build a channel partner programme from scratch for a new launch?"
    look: "Selects and onboards partners carefully, sets clear and fair commission rules, trains them on the project and its RERA details, and tracks the quality of leads as well as the volume."
  - q: "Describe the hardest sale you've closed. What made it difficult, and what was the breakthrough?"
    look: "Finds the buyer's real concern and resolves it honestly (finance, timing or documents), and never closes a sale by misleading the buyer."
greenFlags:
  - "Built a channel partner network and tracked conversion by partner"
  - "Turned old leads into bookings with a specific campaign"
  - "Knows the inventory, average deal size and conversion rate of their current project"
redFlags:
  - "Has worked only in resale or brokerage, for a role selling a developer's new projects"
  - "Can't explain a developer's and a buyer's obligations under RERA"
  - "Leaves salespeople to work their own leads with no oversight or coaching"
