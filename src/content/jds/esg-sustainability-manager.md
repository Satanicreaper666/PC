title: "ESG and sustainability manager"
description: "ESG and sustainability manager job description: ESG strategy, BRSR reporting, emissions accounting, climate risk, supplier audits and ESG ratings."

function: "Strategy and leadership"
level: Manager
experience: "5–9 years"
stage: recruit
checked: 2026-11-02
summary: "This role builds the company's environmental, social and governance (ESG) programme and its reporting. Success means accurate, timely disclosures, measurable progress on the issues that matter most to the business, and sustainability built into operations rather than added at the end."
responsibilities:
  - "Develop and implement the company's ESG strategy, materiality assessment and annual reporting roadmap aligned to GRI, BRSR and SASB frameworks"
  - "Own the BRSR (Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting) filing for listed entities; manage disclosure timelines and SEBI compliance"
  - "Build the climate-risk assessment and transition plan, using frameworks such as TCFD or the ISSB standards, and the Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions inventory"
  - "Partner with procurement on supplier ESG audits, responsible sourcing frameworks and supply chain due diligence"
  - "Engage with ESG rating agencies (MSCI, Sustainalytics, CDP): manage data submissions and improve scores"
  - "Measure and report diversity, inclusion and other social indicators"
  - "Develop employee sustainability engagement programmes and green office initiatives"
competencies:
  - { name: "ESG reporting (GRI, BRSR, SASB)", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Carbon accounting (Scope 1, 2 and 3)", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Climate risk (TCFD or ISSB)", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Stakeholder and investor communication", type: Behavioural, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Supply chain sustainability", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "SEBI BRSR compliance", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
mustHave:
  - "Has worked in ESG, sustainability or CSR"
  - "Knows the BRSR and BRSR Core requirements and SEBI's disclosure rules for listed companies"
  - "Has used the GHG Protocol for emissions accounting"
  - "Has improved an ESG rating or disclosure score and can explain how"
goodToHave:
  - "A sustainability reporting certification, for example from GRI"
  - "Experience with science-based targets"
  - "Experience in manufacturing, energy or financial services"
interview:
  - q: "How would you set up BRSR reporting for a mid-sized listed manufacturer that has never filed?"
    look: "Maps the required disclosures to data owners, sets up collection and controls, closes the biggest gaps first, plans assurance where needed and works back from the filing date."
  - q: "What are the most material ESG issues for a fast-fashion brand in India, and how would you address them?"
    look: "Names issues such as working conditions in the supply chain, water and chemicals, and waste, ranks them with evidence and proposes practical, measurable actions."
  - q: "How would you make the case for a Scope 3 reduction investment to a CFO focused on short-term profit?"
    look: "Speaks the CFO's language: costs, risks, customer and lender requirements, and phased options with clear returns or reduced risk."
greenFlags:
  - "Has led a BRSR filing from scratch"
  - "Links ESG improvements to conversations with investors or lenders"
  - "Built a supplier sustainability audit with measured improvement"
redFlags:
  - "Treats ESG as reporting and PR, with no link to operations"
  - "Can't explain the difference between BRSR and CSR obligations under Indian law"
  - "Doesn't understand the GHG Protocol's emission scopes"
