  - "Own a portfolio of 25–50 business accounts and drive adoption, engagement and renewal"
  - "Run quarterly business reviews that link product use to each customer's goals"
  - "Spot expansion opportunities and work with sales on upgrades and add-ons"
  - "Watch health signals such as log-ins, feature adoption and support tickets, and act before an account is at risk"
  - "Represent customers inside the company: pass feedback to product and back feature requests with data"
  - "Manage renewals, including commercial discussions, and escalate complex contract terms"
  - "Keep account notes and health scores up to date in the CRM"

mustHave:

  - "Writes and speaks clearly enough to present to a customer's senior leaders"

  - "Experience with Gainsight, Totango or a similar platform"

interview:
  - q: "Walk me through a customer who was about to leave. What did you find, and what did you do?"

  - q: "How would you run a quarterly business review for an account that hasn't fully adopted the product?"
    look: "Leads with the customer's goals and data, agrees a small adoption plan with owners and dates, and avoids turning the meeting into a sales pitch."
  - q: "What signals tell you an account is ready to buy more?"
    look: "Names specific usage and business signals, checks them with the customer and brings in sales at the right time."

  - "Can explain their portfolio's net revenue retention and what moved it"
redFlags:
  - "Defines success as customers not complaining"
