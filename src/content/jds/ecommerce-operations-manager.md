summary: "This role makes sure online orders are picked, packed, delivered and, when needed, returned quickly and accurately. Success means on-time delivery, fewer failed deliveries and returns, and a lower cost per shipment."
responsibilities:
  - "Own end-to-end order management: order processing, picking/packing SLAs, dispatch and delivery turnaround time"
  - "Manage 3PL and courier partner relationships: SLA negotiation, performance review and escalation management"
  - "Optimise returns processing and reverse logistics: classification, refund turnaround time and restock rates"
  - "Work with the tech team on the order management system, the warehouse management system and the seller portal"
  - "Track on-time delivery, failed delivery attempts (NDR), return-to-origin (RTO) rates and courier performance"
  - "Drive cost-per-shipment optimisation: packaging, courier mix and zone-wise routing logic"
  - "Plan operations for big sale events and seasonal spikes in demand"
competencies:
  - { name: "Order and fulfilment management", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "3PL and courier partner management", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Returns and reverse logistics", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Order and warehouse management systems", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Data analysis and SLA tracking", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Vendor negotiation", type: Behavioural, level: "Proficient" }
mustHave:
  - "Has worked in e-commerce operations, including at least two years running fulfilment"
  - "Has managed courier and logistics partners against service levels at 1,000 or more orders a day"
  - "Uses order and warehouse management systems such as Unicommerce or Vinculum"
  - "Resolves problems that cut across tech, commercial and customer service teams"
goodToHave:
  - "Experience running a dark store or quick-commerce node"
  - "Experience with cross-border e-commerce logistics"
  - "Lean or Six Sigma training"
interview:
  - q: "Your return-to-origin rate jumped to 18% this month. What do you do in the first 72 hours?"
    look: "Breaks the rate down by pin code, courier, payment type and product, finds the main driver, makes quick fixes (address checks, cash-on-delivery confirmation, courier changes) and sets up monitoring."
  - q: "How do you prepare operations for a sale with five times the normal order volume?"
    look: "Plans capacity with partners well ahead, staffs and trains for the peak, tests systems, sets up a control room and agrees escalation rules."
  - q: "Tell me about a courier partner that kept missing service levels. How did you handle it?"
    look: "Uses data to make the case, agrees a recovery plan with dates, moves volume if it fails and protects customers throughout."
greenFlags:
  - "Cut failed deliveries or return-to-origin rates with a specific change"
  - "Can explain the courier mix they built and its trade-offs between cost and service"
  - "Has run a peak sale without missing service levels"
redFlags:
  - "Treats courier management as administration, with no negotiation or ownership of service levels"
  - "Can't explain how return-to-origin rates affect margin"
  - "Has no e-commerce order management experience, for a role that depends on it"
