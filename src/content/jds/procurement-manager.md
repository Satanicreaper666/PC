title: "Procurement manager"
description: "Procurement manager job description for manufacturing: strategic sourcing, supplier selection and development, negotiation, category plans and savings."
industry: "Manufacturing"
function: "Operations and supply chain"
level: Manager
experience: "6–10 years"
stage: recruit
checked: 2026-11-02
summary: "This role buys the materials, components and services a manufacturer needs at the right cost, quality and risk. Success means agreed savings delivered, reliable suppliers and no surprises from depending on a single source."
responsibilities:
  - "Manage strategic sourcing for direct and indirect materials, and own the yearly savings target"
  - "Lead supplier selection, qualification and performance reviews: on-time in-full delivery, defect rates and commercial terms"
  - "Develop category strategies for key spend buckets: raw materials, components, services and capex"
  - "Negotiate commercial agreements: pricing, payment terms, quality obligations and force majeure provisions"
  - "Drive supplier development: capacity building, localisation and dual-source risk mitigation"
  - "Ensure GST compliance, vendor registration and purchase order documentation accuracy"
  - "Partner with finance on working capital optimisation: payment terms, early payment discounts and vendor financing programmes"
competencies:
  - { name: "Strategic sourcing and negotiation", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Supplier development and performance", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Category management", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Contract law and commercial terms", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "ERP (SAP MM)", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Cost analysis and total cost of ownership", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Stakeholder influence", type: Behavioural, level: "Proficient" }
mustHave:
  - "Has worked in procurement for a manufacturing company, with clear responsibility for categories of spend"
  - "Can show savings they delivered and how"
  - "Works confidently in SAP MM, Ariba or a similar system"
  - "Has built make-or-buy and localisation business cases"
goodToHave:
  - "A procurement qualification such as CIPS, CPSM or CSCP"
  - "Experience with supplier ESG audits and responsible sourcing"
  - "Experience with imports, customs classification and export-import procedures"
interview:
  - q: "Walk me through a sourcing exercise you led for a high-value category. How did you choose suppliers and negotiate?"
    look: "Starts with spend and requirements analysis, uses clear selection criteria, creates real competition, negotiates on total cost and records the decision."
  - q: "How do you manage a sole supplier who knows they have leverage?"
    look: "Builds alternatives over time, understands what the supplier values, uses longer commitments or joint improvements, and protects supply in the meantime."
  - q: "Describe a cost reduction that needed other teams to agree. Where was the resistance, and how did you handle it?"
    look: "Brings engineering, quality and finance in early, uses data on cost and risk, deals with concerns about quality and shares the credit."
greenFlags:
  - "Added a second source for a critical part without disrupting supply"
  - "Ran a supplier development programme that cut defect rates by a measured amount"
  - "Explains total cost of ownership analysis clearly"
redFlags:
  - "Describes procurement as placing purchase orders and chasing delivery"
  - "Can't describe a negotiation that created value beyond a lower price"
  - "Has no approach to supplier risk"
