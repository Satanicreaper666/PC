summary: "This role leads risk management and regulatory compliance for a bank, NBFC or fintech, and is the main contact for its regulators. Success means clean inspections, a risk appetite the board has approved and business teams who involve compliance early."
responsibilities:
  - "Lead enterprise risk management across credit, market, operational and compliance risk"
  - "Own the relationship with regulators such as RBI, SEBI or IRDAI, including inspections, audits and correspondence"
  - "Develop the risk appetite statement with the business and present it to the board risk committee every quarter"
  - "Build and lead a team of 8–12 risk and compliance professionals"
  - "Run anti-money-laundering and KYC policies under the Prevention of Money-laundering Act, 2002 and the regulator's directions"
  - "Advise product and technology teams on data protection under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (most duties apply from May 2027)"
  - "Lead compliance testing, internal audits and tracking of corrective actions"

mustHave:
  - "Has led risk or compliance work at a bank, NBFC or fintech, including managing people"
  - "Knows at least two regulatory regimes in depth, for example RBI and SEBI"
  - "Has presented to board, audit or risk committees"
  - "Has handled a regulatory inspection from preparation to closing the findings"
goodToHave:
  - "A professional qualification such as CA, FRM or CFA, or a law degree"
  - "Experience with regulators outside India"
  - "Has built a compliance function at a growing fintech"
interview:
  - q: "Describe the most serious regulatory challenge your organisation faced. What did you do, in what order, and what was the outcome?"
    look: "Gives a clear sequence: contain the issue, inform the regulator and the board, fix the root causes. Names their own decisions and is open about what went wrong."
  - q: "How do you build trust with business leaders who see compliance as a speed bump?"
    look: "Gets involved early, explains the reason behind a rule and offers workable options, with an example where this changed a product or process for the better."
  - q: "How do you keep your organisation ready for changes in RBI's rules on digital lending?"
    look: "Describes a routine for tracking circulars, assessing impact, assigning owners and testing changes, rather than trying to predict specific future rules."
greenFlags:
  - "Has led an inspection that closed without a major finding and can explain what made the difference"
  - "Business leaders describe them as someone who helps find a compliant way forward"
  - "Explains each risk in terms of its commercial consequence"
redFlags:
  - "Sees compliance mainly as saying no"
  - "Can't name the rule behind a requirement they insist on"
  - "Has never managed people, for a role that leads a team"
