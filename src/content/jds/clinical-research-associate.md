title: "Clinical research associate"
description: "Clinical research associate (CRA) job description: monitor trial sites, verify source data, protect participants and report adverse events on time."

level: Entry
experience: "1–4 years"
stage: recruit
checked: 2026-11-02
summary: "This role monitors clinical trial sites so that the data they collect is accurate and trial participants are protected. Success means clean, verifiable data, sites that follow the protocol and safety events reported on time."
responsibilities:
  - "Conduct site qualification, initiation, monitoring and closeout visits for Phase II/III clinical trials at investigator sites"
  - "Verify GCP compliance, protocol adherence and informed consent processes at each site visit"
  - "Perform source data verification (SDV) and source data review (SDR); document findings in monitoring visit reports"
  - "Train and support investigator site staff on study procedures, data collection and adverse event reporting"
  - "Resolve data queries in electronic data capture (EDC) systems such as Medidata Rave or Veeva Vault"
  - "Check that the investigational product is stored, issued and accounted for correctly at each site"
  - "Report serious adverse events (SAEs) per protocol timelines; support sponsor safety reporting processes"

  - { name: "ICH Good Clinical Practice", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Source data verification", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "EDC systems (Medidata, Veeva)", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Site management", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Adverse event reporting", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Attention to detail", type: Behavioural, level: "Proficient" }
mustHave:
  - "A degree in pharmacy, life sciences or clinical research"
  - "Has carried out on-site monitoring visits"
  - "Knows ICH Good Clinical Practice (GCP) and India's rules on clinical trials"
  - "Has used at least one EDC system to resolve queries"
  - "Can travel to sites across India for much of the month (say how much in your post)"
goodToHave:
  - "A CRA certification such as ACRP or SoCRA"
  - "Experience in oncology, neurology or rare disease trials"
  - "Experience with decentralised trial technology"
interview:
  - q: "Walk me through a routine monitoring visit, from arriving at the site to the final report."
    look: "Covers preparation, consent checks, source data verification, investigational product checks, safety events and follow-up of earlier findings, and writes clear actions with owners."
  - q: "A site coordinator tells you some source documents are missing. What do you do?"
    look: "Finds out what is missing and why, assesses the effect on data and participants, records a deviation where needed, tells the sponsor and agrees corrective actions."
  - q: "How do you handle a principal investigator who resists corrective actions after a finding?"
    look: "Stays respectful and factual, explains the requirement and the risk to participants and data, offers practical help and escalates through the sponsor if needed."
greenFlags:
  - "Can explain how a monitoring visit report is structured"
  - "Has resolved a significant protocol deviation at a site"
  - "Understands how adverse events are graded and why the grade matters"
redFlags:
  - "Has never carried out a monitoring visit without a supervisor, for a role that works alone"
  - "Can't explain how they would classify and report a protocol deviation"
  - "Doesn't know what informed consent requires"
