summary: "This role owns a regulated financial product, such as lending, payments, insurance or investments, used by large numbers of Indian customers. Success means a product people can use and trust, launched and changed within the rules set by its regulator."
responsibilities:
  - "Own the product roadmap for a core fintech product (lending, payments, insurance or investment) serving millions of Indian users"
  - "Define product requirements that balance user experience, business model and regulatory compliance (RBI, SEBI or IRDAI)"
  - "Work with legal and compliance to build regulatory requirements, KYC and data protection into the product from the start (most duties under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 apply from May 2027)"
  - "Research how different groups of Indian users behave, including first-time borrowers, regional-language users and people in smaller cities"
  - "Instrument and monitor product metrics: activation, repayment rate, NPS, CAC, LTV"
  - "Work with partner banks and NBFCs on co-lending, API integration and regulatory reporting"
  - "Lead product launch processes including RBI/SEBI filings, risk committee approvals and app store rollouts"
competencies:
  - { name: "Fintech product design", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Regulatory compliance (RBI/SEBI/IRDAI)", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "User research with first-time digital users", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "API integration and technical literacy", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Data analytics", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Stakeholder management", type: Behavioural, level: "Expert" }
mustHave:
  - "Has managed products, including at least two years in a regulated fintech"
  - "Understands the RBI, SEBI or IRDAI rules that apply to the product they built"
  - "Has designed for first-time or non-metro digital users"
  - "Has shipped products that met measurable business goals"
goodToHave:
  - "A CA or finance background"
  - "Experience with Account Aggregator, UPI or ONDC integrations"
  - "Experience with microfinance, buy-now-pay-later or rural lending products"
interview:
  - q: "How would you redesign the loan application for a first-time borrower in a small town with no credit history?"
    look: "Starts with research on the user's situation, cuts steps, explains costs and terms clearly, uses alternative data within the rules, and designs for trust and informed consent."
  - q: "RBI issues a circular that restricts a core feature of your product. What do you do in the first 24 hours?"
    look: "Reads the circular with compliance, works out the impact and deadlines, informs leaders, plans a compliant change and communicates calmly with customers and partners."
  - q: "How do you balance speed of innovation with the compliance needs of a regulated product?"
    look: "Involves compliance early, separates what the rules fix from what can be tested, and gives an example where this let the team move quickly without breaking rules."
greenFlags:
  - "Has changed a live product to meet a new RBI rule without hurting the user experience"
  - "Understands how Indian fintech products reach people: assisted onboarding, regional-language screens and messaging apps"
  - "Has researched and designed for users outside the big cities"
redFlags:
  - "Copies products from other markets without adapting them to Indian rules and customers"
  - "Has worked only on internal tools, for a role that serves consumers"
  - "Can't explain why a product decision needed regulatory sign-off"
