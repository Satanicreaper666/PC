interview:
  - q: "Walk me through the most complex credit proposal you prepared. What made it hard?"

  - q: "How do you handle a client when you have bad news, such as a rate increase or a declined renewal?"
    look: "Tells the client early and directly, explains the reason, offers options where possible and protects the relationship without making promises the bank can't keep."

    look: "Reasons from evidence such as demand, input costs, debt levels and policy changes, and links the view to how they would watch their own portfolio."
