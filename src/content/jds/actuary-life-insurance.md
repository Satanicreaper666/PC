level: Senior

checked: 2026-11-02
summary: "This role prices new life insurance products and keeps reserves at the right level. Success means products that are priced soundly and approved without delay, reserves that stand up to review, and clear advice to the appointed actuary and the board."
responsibilities:
  - "Design, price and file new insurance products with IRDAI; own actuarial memoranda and pricing models"
  - "Maintain and update reserving models for life and health portfolios, and present results to the appointed actuary every quarter"
  - "Conduct liability adequacy testing, embedded value calculations and sensitivity analyses"
  - "Support reinsurance structuring: quota share, surplus and catastrophe treaties"
  - "Contribute to the asset-liability management (ALM) framework and advise the investment committee on duration matching"
  - "Take part in regulatory filings, correspondence with IRDAI and industry working groups"
  - "Mentor junior actuarial students and manage the actuarial study support programme"
competencies:
  - { name: "Product pricing and filing", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Reserving and liability valuation", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "IRDAI regulations", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Reinsurance structuring", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "ALM and investment advisory", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Actuarial modelling (Prophet, MoSes)", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
mustHave:
  - "A Fellow of the Institute of Actuaries of India, or well advanced towards Fellowship"
  - "Has priced products and set reserves in life insurance"
  - "Builds and maintains models in Prophet, AXIS or a similar actuarial system"
  - "Has presented to the appointed actuary and to board risk or audit committees"
goodToHave:
  - "Fellowship of the Institute and Faculty of Actuaries (UK)"
  - "Experience pricing health insurance products"
  - "Experience with IFRS 17 or Ind AS 117"
interview:
  - q: "How would you approach pricing a new unit-linked product for Indian customers?"
    look: "Sets out the assumptions (mortality, persistency, expenses and investment return), the regulator's limits on charges, profit testing and sensitivities, and checks that the product meets customers' needs."
  - q: "How do you keep pricing work on track through IRDAI's product approval process?"
    look: "Knows the current filing route for the product type, prepares documentation early, anticipates the regulator's questions and keeps the business informed about timelines."
  - q: "Describe the most complex reserving problem you've faced. What did it mean for the results and for the business?"
    look: "Explains the technical issue, the options and the judgement they made, and translates the effect into profit, capital and pricing terms."
greenFlags:
  - "Has taken a new product structure through IRDAI approval and can explain the process"
  - "Explains technical ideas, such as gross and net premium valuation, to people who aren't actuaries"
  - "Has helped actuarial students through their exams"
redFlags:
  - "Has worked on one product line only and can't reason about others"
  - "Can't explain the business effect of a change in reserves"
  - "Ignores commercial drivers, such as distribution and customer needs, when designing a product"
