summary: "This role runs the company's operations day to day so that the founder and CEO can focus on direction, product and capital. Success means plans that turn into results, functional heads who deliver, and customers who get what was promised."
responsibilities:
  - "Own the operating model: define the process architecture, OKR rhythm and governance structure for a 200–1,000 person organisation"
  - "Drive cross-functional P&L ownership: work with finance, revenue and product to ensure every team understands and owns their contribution to unit economics"
  - "Lead the annual and quarterly planning process: goal setting, resource allocation and board reporting"
  - "Lead and develop the functional heads: regular one-to-ones, performance reviews and succession plans"
  - "Own the customer experience and operational SLAs; escalation authority for any service or delivery failure"
  - "Build the data and analytics infrastructure: ensure every team is running their function from a shared source of truth"
  - "Partner with the CEO on fundraising, investor relations and board management: own the board data pack and monthly investor updates"

  - { name: "OKR and performance management", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Executive team development", type: Behavioural, level: "Expert" }

mustHave:
  - "Has held senior operating roles, including several years as COO, general manager or similar with full P&L accountability"
  - "Has helped an organisation through rapid growth, for example from 200 to 1,000 or more people"
  - "Has led several functions, not just one"
  - "Has worked closely with a board and investors"
goodToHave:
  - "Founder or co-founder experience"
  - "Has been part of a later-stage fundraise or an acquisition"
  - "Has served as a board member or observer"
interview:
  - q: "How would you diagnose and fix a company with strong revenue growth but worsening unit economics? What would you do in the first 30 days?"
    look: "Gets to the numbers fast (by product, channel and cohort), separates structural causes from fixable ones, picks a few decisive actions and aligns leaders around them."
  - q: "What operating rhythm would you build for a 500-person company: meetings, reviews and dashboards?"
    look: "Proposes a light, connected set of routines with clear purposes and decision rights, and says what they'd drop as well as what they'd add."
  - q: "Tell me about a major cross-team initiative that nearly failed. What did you do, and what happened?"
    look: "Names their specific intervention, how they rebuilt alignment and accountability, and is honest about the result and the lesson."
greenFlags:
  - "Built a goal-setting system that changed how people worked, not just how they reported"
  - "Managed the exit of a senior functional head and rebuilt the team without disruption"
  - "CEOs they have worked with describe specific results they delivered"
redFlags:
  - "Has worked within a single department only, for a role that spans the company"
  - "Describes the COO role as support rather than ownership"
  - "Can't explain the unit economics of this company after the numbers have been shared with them"
