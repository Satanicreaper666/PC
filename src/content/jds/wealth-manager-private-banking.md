summary: "This role advises high-net-worth clients and families on their investments and long-term financial plans. Success means clients whose goals are met through suitable, well-documented advice, and steady growth in the assets the team looks after."
responsibilities:
  - "Manage and grow a portfolio of 40–80 high-net-worth and ultra-high-net-worth clients"
  - "Conduct comprehensive financial planning: goal mapping, asset allocation, tax efficiency and estate planning"
  - "Advise across asset classes: equity, debt, PMS, AIFs, real estate, insurance and offshore products"
  - "Collaborate with investment desk, product teams and legal on bespoke solutions for ultra-HNI clients"
  - "Win 6–10 new client relationships a year through referrals, events and professional networks"
  - "Make sure every recommendation is suitable and documented: KYC, risk profiling and the SEBI rules that apply to your firm's advice or distribution"
  - "Participate in client education events, investment summits and family office engagements"
competencies:
  - { name: "Wealth planning and asset allocation", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Investment products (PMS, AIF, MF)", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }

  - { name: "Tax planning (Indian and cross-border)", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Regulatory compliance (SEBI)", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Business development and referrals", type: Behavioural, level: "Expert" }
mustHave:
  - "Has advised high-net-worth clients in wealth management, private banking or financial advice"
  - "Can show how the assets they looked after grew, and how much came from new relationships"
  - "Holds the NISM certifications your firm's activities need, for example for mutual fund distribution or investment advice"
  - "Explains portfolio management services (PMS), alternative investment funds (AIFs) and mutual funds clearly, including their risks and costs"
goodToHave:
  - "A professional qualification such as CFP, CWM or CFA"
  - "Experience with family offices and succession planning"
  - "Knows offshore investment routes such as GIFT City and the Liberalised Remittance Scheme"
interview:
  - q: "How would you build a portfolio for a 45-year-old business owner with ₹15 crore to invest over ten years?"
    look: "Asks about goals, liquidity needs, risk profile, tax position and existing assets before naming products, then explains the allocation, the costs and how it will be reviewed."
  - q: "How do you handle a client who wants to sell everything after a 20% fall in their portfolio?"
    look: "Listens first, goes back to the client's goals and plan, explains the options and their consequences, and records the client's decision without pressuring them either way."
  - q: "Describe the most complex family wealth situation you've handled."
    look: "Protects confidentiality, brings in tax and legal specialists where needed, and balances family members' interests within the rules."
greenFlags:
  - "Can show how assets under management grew and explain what drove it"
  - "Kept a client invested through a sharp market fall by helping them stick to a sound plan"
  - "Explains a tax-efficient plan in plain words and says when the client needs a tax adviser"
redFlags:
  - "Can't explain the difference between a PMS and an AIF, in rules or in product terms"
  - "Recommends the same asset class or product to almost every client"
  - "Can't point to new client relationships they built in recent years"
