summary: "This role leads recruitment for technical and business roles and manages a small team of recruiters. Success means good hires in reasonable time, a fair, structured process that hiring managers actually use, and candidates who are treated well whether or not they're hired."
responsibilities:
  - "Own the full-cycle recruitment function for technical and business roles across all levels; manage a team of 4–8 recruiters"
  - "Build and execute sourcing strategy: direct sourcing, employee referrals, LinkedIn, niche job boards and recruitment agencies"
  - "Design and maintain interview processes: structured interview guides, scorecard calibration and debrief facilitation"
  - "Track time to fill, offer acceptance, quality of hire, sources of hire and the diversity of the candidate pool"
  - "Partner with HRBPs and hiring managers on workforce planning, headcount approvals and JD quality"
  - "Build the employer brand: campus relations, the careers page, replies to employer reviews and the candidate experience"
  - "Own the applicant tracking system (ATS): set-up, pipeline visibility and reporting"
  - "Handle candidates' personal data with care: collect only what you need, keep it secure and delete it when it's no longer needed (most duties under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 apply from May 2027)"

mustHave:
  - "Has worked in talent acquisition, including at least two years leading recruiters"
  - "Has hired for both technical roles, such as engineering or data, and business roles"
  - "Uses an ATS and reports hiring data confidently"
  - "Can show how they cut time to fill or raised offer acceptance, and what they changed"
goodToHave:
  - "Experience with campus hiring and graduate programmes"
  - "Has built a recruitment function at a growing company"
  - "Has designed a programme to widen the candidate pool, for example for women returning to work or people with disabilities"
interview:
  - q: "How would you build recruitment for a company growing from 100 to 500 people in 18 months?"
    look: "Starts with the hiring plan and priority roles, sets up the team, ATS, interview process and data, balances sourcing channels and builds in fairness and a good candidate experience from the start."
  - q: "A key engineering role has been open for 90 days. How do you diagnose the problem, and what do you do first?"
    look: "Looks at the funnel stage by stage, checks the brief, pay range and interview process with the hiring manager, and fixes the step that is failing."
  - q: "How do you coach a hiring manager who keeps rejecting good candidates without clear reasons?"
    look: "Uses evidence from scorecards, agrees the must-haves again, introduces structured scoring and keeps the conversation respectful but firm."
greenFlags:
  - "Solved a sourcing problem without adding recruiters"
  - "Knows their offer acceptance rate and the changes that moved it"
  - "Designed a structured interview process that hiring managers kept using"
redFlags:
  - "Describes recruitment as sourcing and scheduling"
  - "Can't describe what a poor hire cost their organisation"
  - "Has never challenged a hiring manager about an unfair or inconsistent interview process"
