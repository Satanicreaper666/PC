  - q: "Using these assumptions, build a simple unit economics model for our business in 15 minutes."
    look: "Lays out revenue and cost per unit clearly, checks the logic, flags the assumptions that matter most and explains what would change the answer. Provide the data and keep the exercise short."

    look: "Uses public information well, reasons clearly, picks one risk and proposes practical first steps, while acknowledging what they can't know from outside."
