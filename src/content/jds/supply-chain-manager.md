title: "Supply chain manager"
description: "Supply chain manager job description for manufacturing: S&OP, inventory across plants, supplier management, logistics and cost reduction."
industry: "Manufacturing"
function: "Operations and supply chain"
level: Manager
experience: "6–10 years"
stage: recruit
checked: 2026-11-02
summary: "This role runs the supply chain for two or three plants, from planning and buying to inventory and dispatch. Success means reliable supply to customers, less inventory for the same service and steady cost reductions."
responsibilities:
  - "Own the supply chain for 2–3 plants: procurement, production planning, inventory and outbound logistics"
  - "Run sales and operations planning (S&OP): demand forecasts, supply plans and agreement among leaders"
  - "Manage a base of 50–150 suppliers, including supplier development and risk planning"
  - "Set inventory levels for raw material, work in progress and finished goods"
  - "Work with finance on working capital and with manufacturing on capacity"
  - "Lead cost reduction through value engineering, lean projects and renegotiation, against agreed yearly targets"
  - "Keep GST, e-way bill and import and export documentation accurate"
competencies:
  - { name: "S&OP and demand planning", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Lean and Six Sigma", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Supplier management", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "ERP systems (for example SAP MM or PP)", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Negotiation", type: Behavioural, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Leading across functions", type: Behavioural, level: "Proficient" }
mustHave:
  - "Has managed supply chain in a manufacturing company, including planning and inventory across more than one site"
  - "Has run or played a major part in an S&OP process"
  - "Works confidently in SAP or a similar ERP"
  - "Knows GST, e-way bill and customs documentation in practice"
goodToHave:
  - "Lean or Six Sigma certification"
  - "Experience with IATF 16949 or AS9100 quality systems"
  - "Has worked on supply chain digitisation"
interview:
  - q: "Describe the biggest supply disruption you managed. How did you decide what to protect first?"
    look: "Uses a clear priority rule (customers, margin, safety stock), communicates early and adds lasting resilience such as second sources or new buffer levels."
  - q: "How do you build an S&OP process when sales and operations don't trust each other's numbers?"
    look: "Creates one shared set of data, a regular meeting where decisions are recorded, and accountability for forecast accuracy on both sides."
  - q: "How would you approach a 10% reduction in the cost of goods for next year?"
    look: "Breaks the cost into its drivers, sizes options across materials, logistics, yield and design, protects quality and service, and sets owners and milestones."
greenFlags:
  - "Has measurably improved inventory turns or supplier lead times"
  - "Has handled a supply disruption and put lasting safeguards in place"
  - "Talks about cost and service trade-offs together"
redFlags:
  - "Has worked only in procurement or only in logistics, for a role that needs both"
  - "Can't explain how inventory decisions affect working capital"
  - "Has never developed a supplier"
