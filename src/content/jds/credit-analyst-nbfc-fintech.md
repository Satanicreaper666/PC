summary: "This role assesses whether retail, small-business or corporate borrowers can repay, and recommends whether and how to lend. Success means sound decisions made on time, a healthy loan book and clear reasons recorded for every exception to policy."
responsibilities:
  - "Analyse creditworthiness of retail, MSME or corporate borrowers using financial statements, bank statements and bureau data"
  - "Prepare credit assessment memos with risk recommendations; present to credit committee"
  - "Assess cash flow, collateral quality, repayment capacity and industry risk for each application"
  - "Identify early warning signals in existing portfolios; flag accounts for enhanced monitoring"
  - "Contribute to policy development: underwriting criteria, deviation matrix and credit scoring model inputs"
  - "Make decisions within the agreed turnaround times without cutting corners on quality"
  - "Follow the RBI rules that apply to the lender, report accurately to credit bureaus and keep loan documentation complete"

mustHave:
  - "Has worked in credit underwriting at a bank, NBFC or fintech"
  - "Analyses profit and loss statements, balance sheets and bank statements across different industries"
  - "Has used credit bureau reports and bank statement analysis tools"
  - "Understands RBI's prudential norms, including asset classification and provisioning"
goodToHave:
  - "A CA or a finance qualification"
  - "Experience with machine-learning-assisted underwriting"
  - "Experience with GST-based lending or Account Aggregator data"
  - "A credit certification such as CCRA"
interview:
  - q: "Here is a set of financial statements. Talk me through your assessment of this borrower in ten minutes."
    look: "Looks at cash flow and repayment capacity first, checks leverage, working capital and related-party items, spots inconsistencies and reaches a clear recommendation with conditions."
  - q: "How would you find early warning signs in a portfolio of 5,000 small-business loans?"
    look: "Uses data such as repayment behaviour, bureau changes and bank statement or GST trends, sets clear triggers and explains what happens when an account is flagged."
  - q: "Describe a credit decision you made that went wrong. What did you miss, and what did you learn?"
    look: "Owns the decision, explains the signals they missed without excuses, and shows how it changed their checks or the policy."
greenFlags:
  - "Spots warning signs in a set of accounts quickly and explains them"
  - "Can point to a policy change they recommended and its effect on the portfolio"
  - "Keeps up with RBI circulars and can explain one that changed their work"
redFlags:
  - "Relies only on a model's score, with no judgement of their own"
  - "Can't explain why a borrower with a good bureau score might still be a poor risk"
  - "Has never written up the reasons for an exception to policy"
