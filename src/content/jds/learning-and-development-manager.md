function: "HR and people"
level: Manager
experience: "5–8 years"
stage: recruit
checked: 2026-11-02
summary: "This role plans and delivers the company's learning programmes, from onboarding and skills training to leadership development. Success means programmes that change how people work, measured beyond attendance and feedback scores."
responsibilities:
  - "Design and deliver the annual L&D calendar: leadership development, functional skills, onboarding and compliance training"
  - "Run the learning management system (LMS): content, completion tracking and learning data"
  - "Build and facilitate leadership development programmes for manager and senior manager cohorts"
  - "Design custom e-learning modules and blended learning programmes using tools like Articulate Storyline or Rise"
  - "Partner with HRBPs and business heads to identify skill gaps; conduct annual training needs analysis (TNA)"
  - "Manage external training vendor relationships: empanelment, quality review and ROI measurement"
  - "Track and report learning metrics: completion rates, assessment scores, learning hours and post-training behavioural change (Kirkpatrick Levels 3–4)"
competencies:
  - { name: "Instructional design", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Learning management systems", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Facilitation and programme delivery", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Training needs analysis", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Learning analytics", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Vendor management", type: Behavioural, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Executive stakeholder influence", type: Behavioural, level: "Proficient" }
mustHave:
  - "Has worked in L&D, including at least two years owning a full learning calendar"
  - "Has designed programmes from scratch, not only curated or delivered them"
  - "Has run an LMS, including its reporting"
  - "Facilitates confidently with senior managers and directors"
goodToHave:
  - "A talent development certification such as ATD's CPTD"
  - "Experience applying the 70-20-10 model"
  - "Experience working alongside executive coaches"
interview:
  - q: "Walk me through a learning programme you designed from scratch. What was the business problem, how did you design it and how did you measure impact?"
    look: "Starts from a business problem, designs for practice and use on the job, and measures change at work, not only how people rated the session."
  - q: "How do you make a leadership programme different from a standard management course?"
    look: "Uses real challenges from the business, practice with feedback, involvement of participants' managers and follow-up over months rather than a one-off event."
  - q: "How do you win leaders' support for learning that won't show results straight away?"
    look: "Links it to a priority the leader owns, agrees early signs of progress, starts with a small pilot and reports honestly."
greenFlags:
  - "Has measured behaviour change or business results from a programme they designed"
  - "Built a programme that leaders adopted without being told to"
  - "Explains the return on an L&D budget in business terms"
redFlags:
  - "Describes success only as high completion rates and good feedback scores"
  - "Can't show a programme they took from needs analysis to evaluation"
  - "Has delivered only bought-in content, with no design of their own"
