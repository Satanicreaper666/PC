function: "HR and people"
level: Manager
experience: "5–9 years"
stage: recruit
checked: 2026-11-02
summary: "This role designs and runs the company's pay and benefits, from salary bands and yearly increments to variable pay and stock options. Success means pay that is competitive, fair between people doing the same work and fully compliant with the Labour Codes and tax rules."
responsibilities:
  - "Run the yearly pay benchmarking: take part in salary surveys, analyse the data and make recommendations"
  - "Design and maintain salary bands, pay grades and career level frameworks across the organisation"
  - "Administer annual increment cycle, promotion adjustments and off-cycle corrections; partner with HRBPs on exceptions"
  - "Manage ESOP/stock compensation programme: grant administration, vesting schedules, exercise events and employee communication"
  - "Design and administer variable pay programmes: target setting, achievement tracking and payout calculations"
  - "Keep pay compliant: the definition of wages under the Labour Codes, PF, ESI, professional tax, salary TDS under the Income-tax Act, 2025, gratuity and statutory bonus"
  - "Partner with finance on payroll budgeting, headcount cost tracking and compensation cost forecasting"
  - "Check pay equity regularly and fix gaps between people doing the same or similar work; the Code on Wages, 2019 bars pay discrimination on the ground of gender"
competencies:
  - { name: "Compensation benchmarking and survey analysis", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Pay band and grade design", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "ESOP administration", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Indian payroll compliance", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Variable pay design", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Advanced Excel and data analysis", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Executive communication", type: Behavioural, level: "Proficient" }
mustHave:
  - "Has specialised in compensation and benefits"
  - "Has worked hands-on with at least one major salary survey"
  - "Knows Indian payroll law in depth: wages under the Labour Codes, PF, ESI, professional tax, salary TDS, gratuity and bonus"
  - "Builds salary models in spreadsheets and uses an HRIS such as Workday or SuccessFactors"
goodToHave:
  - "Experience designing ESOP pools and running stock plans"
  - "A reward certification such as WorldatWork's CCP"
  - "Experience preparing papers for a board's nomination and remuneration committee"
interview:
  - q: "How would you design salary bands for a company growing from 300 to 1,000 employees?"
    look: "Starts with job levels, sets midpoints from market data, chooses spreads and overlaps with reasons, and plans how to bring existing salaries into the bands fairly."
  - q: "How would you run a pay equity audit? What would you look for, and how would you fix gaps?"
    look: "Compares like-for-like roles, allows for legitimate factors such as level and performance, investigates unexplained gaps by gender and other groups, and budgets fixes over a set period."
  - q: "How would you explain an ESOP's vesting schedule and tax to a new joiner who has never had stock options?"
    look: "Uses plain words and a worked example of grant, vesting, exercise and sale, explains that tax can arise when options are exercised and again when shares are sold, and suggests personal tax advice."
greenFlags:
  - "Has run a pay equity analysis and fixed the gaps it found"
  - "Uses data to explain why the same role may be paid differently in different cities or companies"
  - "Has run an ESOP grant cycle, including the tax treatment when options are exercised"
redFlags:
  - "Sees compensation as administration rather than analysis and design"
  - "Can't explain the difference between CTC and take-home pay for a given salary structure"
  - "Doesn't know how the Labour Codes' 50% rule on wages affects salary structures"
