level: Manager

checked: 2026-11-02
summary: "This role owns the quality management system for a manufacturing site with several product lines. Success means fewer defects and repeat complaints, audits closed without major findings, and quality built into new products before they launch."
responsibilities:
  - "Own and maintain the Quality Management System (QMS) compliant with ISO 9001/IATF 16949/AS9100D as applicable"
  - "Lead internal and external audits (customer, third-party and regulatory) and manage corrective and preventive actions (CAPA) to closure"
  - "Manage incoming quality control (IQC), in-process quality control (IPQC) and final product inspection (FPQ) functions"
  - "Investigate and resolve customer complaints with root cause tools such as 5-why, fishbone and 8D, and report on corrective actions"
  - "Drive Statistical Process Control (SPC) implementation: control charts, capability studies (Cpk) and measurement system analysis (MSA/Gauge R&R)"
  - "Work with new product development on design for manufacture (DFM), design and process FMEAs and control plans"
  - "Build and mentor a quality team of 10–25 QA engineers, inspectors and lab technicians"
competencies:
  - { name: "QMS (ISO 9001/IATF 16949/AS9100D)", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Core quality tools (FMEA, SPC, 8D, APQP)", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Audit management", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Customer quality management", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Six Sigma (Green/Black Belt)", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }

  - { name: "Data-driven problem solving", type: Behavioural, level: "Expert" }
mustHave:
  - "An engineering degree, typically mechanical, metallurgical or production"
  - "Has worked in quality assurance in manufacturing, including leading a team"
  - "Lead auditor certification for ISO 9001 or IATF 16949"
  - "Has used APQP and PPAP to launch products, and handled customer complaints end to end"
goodToHave:
  - "Six Sigma Black Belt"
  - "Automotive experience under IATF 16949, working directly with vehicle makers"
  - "Experience with aerospace (AS9100) or pharmaceutical good manufacturing practice"
interview:
  - q: "Walk me through an 8D report you led for a critical customer complaint. What was the root cause, and what did you change?"
    look: "Contains the problem quickly, uses data to find the real root cause (not just 'operator error'), fixes the system and checks over time that the fix held."
  - q: "How do you build a quality culture when operators are under pressure to meet output targets?"
    look: "Makes it easy and safe to stop the line and report problems, involves operators in fixes, shows leaders backing quality over volume and tracks both together."
  - q: "How would you introduce statistical process control in a plant that has never used it?"
    look: "Starts with a few critical characteristics, checks the measurement system first, trains operators, uses simple charts with clear reaction rules and shows early wins."
greenFlags:
  - "Has closed a major customer quality concern and can explain the root cause and the fix"
  - "Built a statistical process control programme and can show capability before and after"
  - "Can show audit findings that didn't come back"
redFlags:
  - "Sees quality as inspection only, catching defects rather than preventing them"
  - "Can't explain how a gauge repeatability and reproducibility study works"
  - "Can't describe what they changed after a serious customer complaint"
