summary: "This role runs a product category on an online marketplace or retail platform, including its profit and loss, suppliers and range. Success means sales and margin targets met while returns stay low and customers stay satisfied."
responsibilities:
  - "Own the category's profit and loss: sales, margin, returns and customer satisfaction"
  - "Find, onboard and develop suppliers and brands, and negotiate commercial terms"
  - "Set the range: breadth, depth, price points and whether to develop a private label"
  - "Plan promotions and visibility on the platform with the growth team"
  - "Analyse category performance with SQL and a BI tool, and run weekly business reviews"
  - "Track competitors' pricing, range and positioning"
  - "Lead launches for new brands, seasonal events and major campaigns"

mustHave:
  - "Has managed a category in e-commerce, FMCG or retail against sales or margin targets"
  - "Can show measured results against those targets"
  - "Has negotiated with suppliers and can describe the outcome"
  - "Uses spreadsheets at an advanced level and can write basic SQL"
goodToHave:
  - "Experience in fashion, electronics or FMCG categories"
  - "Experience on a large online marketplace or with a direct-to-consumer brand"
  - "Has worked on private label or brand development"
interview:
  - q: "Take me through a category you turned around. What was broken, what did you change and what happened?"
    look: "Diagnoses with data (traffic, conversion, price, availability), picks a few changes with clear reasons and puts numbers on the result."
  - q: "How do you decide which new brands to bring onto the platform?"
    look: "Uses clear criteria such as customer demand, margin, supplier reliability and fit with the range, and checks the decision after launch."
  - q: "Describe a supplier negotiation where you were in a weak position. How did you approach it?"
    look: "Prepares alternatives, understands what the supplier values, trades on more than price and keeps the relationship workable."
