responsibilities:
  - "Oversee clinical operations across 3–8 facilities: outpatient flow, inpatient throughput, operating theatre use and discharge times"
  - "Lead preparation for NABH or JCI accreditation and keep standards up between audits"
  - "Work with the medical director to standardise clinical protocols and care pathways"
  - "Plan the clinical workforce: credentialing, rosters, capacity and cover"
  - "Lead quality and patient safety work: adverse event reviews, root cause analysis and corrective actions"
  - "Track operational measures such as average length of stay, bed occupancy, readmissions and billing turnaround"
  - "Work with insurance and TPA teams to resolve pre-authorisation and cashless claim disputes"

mustHave:
  - "Has run clinical operations for a multi-speciality hospital with direct responsibility for NABH compliance"

  - "A degree in hospital or healthcare administration (MHA or MBA), or equivalent experience"
goodToHave:
  - "JCI accreditation experience"
  - "Six Sigma or similar improvement training"
  - "Experience in a hospital chain"
interview:
  - q: "Describe the most serious patient safety incident you managed. What did you do immediately, and what changed afterwards?"
    look: "Puts the patient first, follows a clear escalation and disclosure process, runs a fair root cause review and names a lasting system change, not just retraining one person."
  - q: "How do you get NABH compliance from clinical teams who see it as paperwork?"
    look: "Links standards to patient outcomes, involves clinicians in designing the process, builds compliance into daily work and checks it with audits."
  - q: "Which five measures would you look at every day to run hospital operations, and why?"
    look: "Chooses a balanced set covering flow, quality, safety and finance, explains what each one tells them and what action a bad number triggers."
greenFlags:

  - "Built a reporting culture where frontline staff raise issues early"
  - "Shows operational improvements with measured turnaround or cost results"
redFlags:
  - "Treats clinical governance as an HR matter"
  - "Has no direct experience with accreditation or quality bodies"
  - "Can't describe how they would keep standards consistent across several sites"
