title: "Technical programme manager"
description: "Technical programme manager job description: deliver cross-team engineering programmes, manage dependencies and risks, and coordinate releases."
industry: "Technology and SaaS"
function: "Engineering and data"
level: Manager
experience: "6–10 years"
stage: recruit
checked: 2026-11-02
summary: "This role delivers complex technical programmes that span several engineering teams and have firm deadlines. Success means programmes that land on time, with risks raised early and decisions made by the right people."
responsibilities:
  - "Deliver complex technical programmes that span several engineering teams"
  - "Define scope, milestones, dependencies and risks, and keep the programme plan current"
  - "Run cross-team alignment through regular syncs, dependency maps and clear escalation paths"
  - "Report programme health to senior leaders: status, blockers and schedule changes"
  - "Coordinate releases, including go or no-go decisions, release notes and stakeholder updates"
  - "Find and fix gaps in how engineering delivers work"
  - "Work with architecture and security teams so that technical standards are met"
competencies:
  - { name: "Programme and project management", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Technical understanding (architecture and code)", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Managing risks and dependencies", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Stakeholder communication", type: Behavioural, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Facilitation", type: Behavioural, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Process design", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
mustHave:
  - "Has managed technical programmes or projects at a software company"
  - "Can discuss technical decisions credibly with architects and senior engineers"
  - "Has run programmes involving four or more engineering teams at once"
  - "Raises schedule risks early and clearly with senior leaders"
goodToHave:
  - "PMP, Scrum or SAFe certification"
  - "A background as a software engineer or architect"
  - "Has led a platform migration or infrastructure modernisation"
interview:
  - q: "Tell me about the most complex cross-team programme you managed. What was the hardest dependency to resolve?"
    look: "Shows how they found the dependency, who they brought together, the trade-off they agreed and how they kept it from slipping again."
  - q: "How do you tell a senior leader about a schedule slip before it becomes a crisis?"
    look: "Raises it early with facts, impact and options, recommends one, and avoids both surprises and false optimism."
  - q: "Describe how you improved an engineering delivery process. What was broken, what did you change and what was the result?"
    look: "Uses data such as lead time or defects, involves the engineers affected and measures the result after the change."
greenFlags:
  - "Can describe a programme that nearly failed and the specific step that saved it"
  - "Maps dependencies before setting milestones"
  - "References from engineers describe them as technically credible"
redFlags:
  - "Focuses on process for its own sake rather than outcomes"
  - "Can't explain a technical trade-off they influenced"
  - "Measures success by meetings held and tickets closed instead of what shipped"
