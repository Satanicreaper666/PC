function: "HR and people"
level: Executive
experience: "18–25 years"
stage: recruit
checked: 2026-11-02
summary: "This role leads the people function and sits on the leadership team, shaping how the company hires, develops, pays and keeps its people. Success means the right leaders in the right roles, a fair and competitive reward system and a culture the business can grow on."
responsibilities:
  - "Set the people strategy for the next three years in line with business plans, and own culture, talent and organisation design"
  - "Lead talent reviews, succession planning and leadership development for the senior team"
  - "Build the employer brand and employee value proposition for campus and experienced hiring"
  - "Set the reward philosophy, the benchmarking routine and the total rewards structure"
  - "Oversee HR operations, including payroll compliance (PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS), POSH compliance and audit readiness under the four Labour Codes"
  - "Advise the board and its committees on executive pay, ESOPs and governance"
  - "Lead the diversity and inclusion strategy, with measurable goals at leadership levels"

mustHave:
  - "Has led HR at a senior level, including a business-facing role such as head of HR business partners"
  - "Has managed an HR team of 20 or more people across several specialisms"
  - "Knows Indian statutory compliance and executive pay structures in depth"
  - "Has worked with a board or its committees on people matters"
goodToHave:
  - "Has been CHRO or head of people at a company of 2,000 or more employees"
  - "Has served on, or reported to, a board's nomination and remuneration committee"
  - "Has led HR across several countries"
interview:
  - q: "How did you build or change the talent strategy in your last organisation? What did you inherit, what did you change and what was the measured impact?"
    look: "Starts from the business need, makes clear choices rather than listing every HR programme, and shows results such as internal fill rates, regretted attrition or time to productivity."
  - q: "Tell me about the hardest people decision you advised a CEO on."
    look: "Weighs fairness, legal risk and business need, gives honest advice even when it is unwelcome and treats the people affected with care. They should not reveal confidential details."
  - q: "How should a CHRO protect the culture while headcount grows quickly?"
    look: "Names specific mechanisms, such as hiring standards, manager capability, onboarding and how decisions are communicated, and how they would measure whether the culture is holding."
greenFlags:
  - "References from CEOs and business leaders, not just HR colleagues"
  - "Explains the business case for every major people investment"
  - "Has led a significant reorganisation and can describe how people were treated through it"
redFlags:
  - "Has worked only in HR centres of excellence, with no business-facing leadership"
  - "Can't describe the competitive position of the industries they have worked in"
  - "Describes culture only as engagement scores and offsites"
