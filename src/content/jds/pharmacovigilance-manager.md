title: "Pharmacovigilance manager"
description: "Pharmacovigilance manager job description: case processing, periodic safety reports, signal detection, labelling and reporting to CDSCO and other regulators."
industry: "Healthcare and pharma"
function: "Healthcare and clinical"
level: Manager
experience: "5–9 years"
stage: recruit
checked: 2026-11-02
summary: "This role runs drug safety monitoring for a set of products, from individual case reports to periodic safety reviews. Success means safety signals found early, every report filed on time and product labels that reflect what is known."
responsibilities:
  - "Manage end-to-end individual case safety report (ICSR) processing: collection, medical evaluation, coding (MedDRA) and regulatory submission"
  - "Oversee Periodic Benefit-Risk Evaluation Reports (PBRERs) and Development Safety Update Reports (DSURs) for assigned products"
  - "Liaise with CDSCO, EMA and FDA on expedited and periodic safety reporting obligations"
  - "Build and maintain the safety database (Argus, ARISg or Oracle AERS); ensure data integrity and compliance"
  - "Conduct signal detection activities: statistical signal methods, literature surveillance and regulatory agency safety communications"
  - "Develop and update product labelling: SmPC, patient information leaflets and local prescribing information"
  - "Train other teams on adverse event reporting, the marketing authorisation holder's duties and CDSCO's pharmacovigilance requirements"
competencies:
  - { name: "ICSR processing and MedDRA coding", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Pharmacovigilance rules (CDSCO, EMA and FDA)", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Safety database (Argus/ARISg)", type: Technical, level: "Expert" }
  - { name: "Signal detection methods", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Labelling and regulatory writing", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
  - { name: "Medical evaluation", type: Technical, level: "Proficient" }
mustHave:
  - "A degree in pharmacy, medicine or dentistry (M.Pharm, MBBS or BDS)"
  - "Has worked in pharmacovigilance at a marketing authorisation holder or a contract research organisation"
  - "Codes cases in MedDRA and processes individual case safety reports to a high standard"
  - "Knows CDSCO's pharmacovigilance requirements and those of at least one other major regulator, such as EMA or the US FDA"
goodToHave:
  - "Pharmacovigilance training from a body such as DIA or TOPRA"
  - "Has prepared periodic safety reports (PBRERs or DSURs) end to end"
  - "Experience with risk management plans"
interview:
  - q: "How would you handle a cluster of unexpected serious adverse events in a phase III trial?"
    look: "Confirms and assesses the cases quickly, informs the safety committee and sponsor, meets expedited reporting timelines, checks for a signal and considers measures to protect participants."
  - q: "How do you run signal detection for a product already on the market?"
    look: "Combines database methods with literature and regulators' communications, sets thresholds, records decisions and explains when a signal leads to a label change."
  - q: "CDSCO has sent a safety query about one of your products. What is your process?"
    look: "Logs it, gathers the data and medical review, agrees the response with regulatory affairs, meets the deadline and tracks any commitments made."
greenFlags:
  - "Has taken a safety signal from detection to regulatory reporting"
  - "Explains the causality assessment methods used for serious cases"
  - "Has represented the company at an inspection or in reply to a regulator's query"
redFlags:
  - "Has processed cases but never done a medical review, for a role that needs one"
  - "Can't explain the difference between an adverse event and an adverse drug reaction"
  - "Doesn't know India's own pharmacovigilance requirements"
