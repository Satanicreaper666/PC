  - "Own and grow a portfolio of enterprise and mid-market B2B accounts; hit revenue and renewal targets"
  - "Manage end-to-end sales cycle: prospecting, discovery, demo, proposal, negotiation and closure"
  - "Conduct training needs analysis (TNA) workshops with HR/L&D teams to align solutions to skill gaps"
  - "Build and present ROI proposals: learning outcome projections, benchmark comparisons and business case structuring"
  - "Manage implementation handoffs to the customer success team with a detailed account transition document"
  - "Partner with the marketing team on ABM campaigns, reference accounts and case study development"
  - "Track pipeline, forecast accuracy and account health in CRM (Salesforce or HubSpot)"

mustHave:

  - "Understands how training budgets are set, approved and measured"

  - "Earlier experience in L&D or HR"

interview:
  - q: "How would you sell a ₹30 lakh annual learning subscription to a 2,000-person manufacturer where the L&D head likes the product but the CFO wants to cut costs?"
    look: "Builds a business case with the L&D head tied to the CFO's priorities, offers a phased or pilot option, uses evidence from similar clients and doesn't over-promise."
  - q: "A buyer asks for return-on-investment data before buying a learning programme. How do you build the case?"
    look: "Agrees measurable outcomes and baselines with the client, uses honest evidence from comparable clients and proposes how results will be tracked."
  - q: "Describe the most complex enterprise deal you've closed. What made it complex, and what was your strategy?"
    look: "Maps the stakeholders, handles procurement and legal, manages the timeline and is clear about their own part."
