responsibilities:
  - "Own the academic vision, curriculum framework and learning outcomes across all programmes and cohorts"
  - "Lead a team of 15–50 educators, curriculum designers and academic coordinators"
  - "Set and monitor learning quality metrics: cohort outcomes, assessment scores, educator NPS and completion rates"
  - "Partner with product to translate pedagogical principles into learning platform features and assessment tools"
  - "Build industry advisory panels: connect curriculum to current market skill requirements"
  - "Lead accreditation and recognition processes: UGC, AICTE or international accreditation bodies as applicable"
  - "Represent the academic brand externally: partnerships, conferences, press and government policy forums"

  - "Has led academics at an online education company or a business school"

interview:
  - q: "How would you redesign a six-month online management programme so that graduates are ready for what employers need?"
    look: "Starts from employer and learner research, defines outcomes, builds practice and assessment around real work and plans how to measure readiness."
  - q: "How do you keep standards high in a self-paced online programme where you can't enforce attendance?"
    look: "Designs engaging, well-paced content, frequent low-stakes assessment, feedback and support, and uses data to spot learners at risk early."
  - q: "Tell me about a difficult decision about an educator who was popular but whose learners weren't learning enough."
    look: "Uses evidence fairly, gives feedback and support first, sets a clear plan and acts in the learners' interest if things don't improve."
