  - "Manage the influencer and creator ecosystem: identify, brief, track and measure ROI from collaborations"
  - "Drive email, WhatsApp and SMS lifecycle marketing using tools like Klaviyo, WebEngage or MoEngage"
  - "Analyse cohort-level unit economics: CAC, LTV, repeat purchase rate and RFM-based segmentation"
  - "Launch new product lines with coordinated brand, PR and performance marketing campaigns"

  - q: "How do you decide the mix of performance, organic and creator marketing for a new product launch?"
    look: "Starts from the audience and the product's margin, runs tests with clear measures and moves budget on evidence rather than habit."

    look: "Owns the result, explains the cause with data, stopped or changed the campaign in time and applied the lesson later."
