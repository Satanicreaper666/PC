summary: "This entry-level sales role at a B2B company researches target accounts, starts conversations and books qualified meetings for account executives. Success means a steady flow of good meetings and accurate records, and the role is a common first step into a sales career."
responsibilities:
  - "Research and identify ideal customer profiles (ICP) from target account lists across SMB and mid-market segments"
  - "Execute multi-channel outbound sequences: cold calls, personalised LinkedIn messages, email and WhatsApp outreach"
  - "Qualify inbound leads with discovery calls and a framework such as BANT or MEDDIC, then hand qualified opportunities to account executives"
  - "Maintain CRM (HubSpot/Salesforce) hygiene: accurate pipeline data, contact information and sequence tracking"
  - "Research each account and contact so that every message is relevant to them"
  - "Meet weekly and monthly KPIs: calls made, emails sent, meetings booked and SQLs generated"
  - "Collaborate with marketing on ICP feedback, campaign response analysis and messaging A/B testing"

mustHave:
  - "Writes clear, specific messages in English, such as a three-line email a busy person would read"
  - "Is comfortable making many calls a day and handling rejection"
  - "Tracks their own numbers and acts on them"
  - "A degree in any subject, or equivalent experience in a customer-facing role"
goodToHave:
  - "A B2B sales internship or an earlier SDR role"
  - "Experience with sales engagement tools such as Apollo or Outreach"
  - "Knows the company's target industry"
interview:
  - q: "How would you research and build an outreach sequence for a logistics company with 500 employees?"
    look: "Finds the right roles to contact, uses specific facts about the company to tailor the message, plans several channels and touches, and says how they'd measure replies."
  - q: "Role-play: I'm head of operations at a manufacturing company. Cold-call me about our supply chain software."
    look: "Opens briefly and relevantly, asks questions before pitching, handles objections calmly and asks for a clear next step. Judge the approach, not the polish: role-plays are stressful for anyone."
  - q: "What do you do when a prospect tells you for the third time that they're not interested?"
    look: "Respects the answer, checks whether the timing or the contact was wrong, records it accurately and moves on rather than pushing harder."
greenFlags:
  - "Comes prepared with research on the company and a draft outreach message"
  - "Has met or beaten an outreach target before, even in a part-time job or internship"
  - "Asks about the ideal customer and the product before asking about targets"
redFlags:
  - "Can't explain how they'd decide whether a prospect is worth pursuing"
  - "Can't write a personalised message in the exercise, even with time to prepare"
  - "Sees the job as filling a pipeline, with no interest in what happens to the leads"
