faq:
  - q: What are the TDS due dates for 2026-27?
    a: Deposit TDS by the 7th of the following month (TDS deducted in March by 30 April). File quarterly statements by 31 July, 31 October, 31 January and 31 May, and give employees their salary TDS certificate (Form 130) by 15 June 2027.
  - q: Has Form 16 been replaced?
    a: Yes, from tax year 2026-27. Under the Income-tax Rules, 2026, the salary TDS certificate is Form 130, the quarterly salary TDS statement is Form 138 (earlier Form 24Q), and employees' claims go in Form 124 (earlier Form 12BB). Form 16 for 2025-26 was due by 15 June 2026.
  - q: When must final wages be paid when someone leaves?
    a: Within two working days of the person's resignation, removal, dismissal or retrenchment (Code on Wages, section 17(2)). For resignations this is commonly read as two working days after the last working day; there is no official clarification yet. Gratuity, if due, is payable within 30 days.
  - q: When must statutory bonus be paid?
    a: Within eight months of the end of the accounting year, so by 30 November for a year that ends on 31 March.
  - q: When is the POSH annual report due?
    a: The Act asks the Internal Committee for a report each calendar year but does not fix one national date. 31 January, for the previous calendar year, is a common deadline; check with your District Officer.

This calendar covers April 2026 to March 2027. It is the first tax year under the Income-tax Act, 2025, and the first full year under the Labour Codes, so several forms and dates are new. The monthly payroll rhythm is shown once; the table then lists what each month adds.

For a monthly wage period, wages must be paid before the end of the 7th day of the following month (Code on Wages, section 17). Paying earlier is fine; paying later is not.

<Callout type="note" title="New form numbers from 2026-27">
The Income-tax Rules, 2026 renamed the salary forms: the TDS certificate is now **Form 130** (was Form 16), the quarterly salary TDS statement is **Form 138** (was Form 24Q), and employees' claims for deductions go in **Form 124** (was Form 12BB). Forms for 2025-26 still use the old names.
</Callout>

## Month by month

| Month | Statutory and payroll | HR work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| April 2026 | Deposit March 2026 TDS by 30 April. New ESI contribution period from 1 April. Confirm each employee's tax regime (the new regime is the default) and collect Form 124 claims. | Revised salaries take effect; send revision letters. Share company and team goals. Publish the training calendar. |
| May 2026 | File the Q4 2025-26 TDS statement (Form 24Q) by 31 May. | Agree individual goals. Run an engagement pulse. Plan campus and intern hiring. |
| June 2026 | Give employees Form 16 for 2025-26 by 15 June. The June welfare fund period closes in half-yearly states. | First-quarter check-ins. Review hiring and attrition against plan. |
| July 2026 | File the Q1 TDS statement (Form 138) by 31 July. | Quarterly people report to leadership. Onboard interns. |
| August 2026 | Monthly cycle only. | Succession review for critical roles. Nominate people for development programmes. |
| September 2026 | PF wage ceiling rose to ₹25,000 a month on 17 September 2026: enrol newly covered staff and split September's PF across both ceilings (return due 15 October). | Mid-year performance reviews. Start workforce planning for 2027-28. |
| October 2026 | File the Q2 TDS statement by 31 October. A new ESI contribution period starts on 1 October; check who is now covered. | Annual engagement survey. Plan festive-season leave and cover. |
| November 2026 | Pay statutory bonus for 2025-26 by 30 November. Old ESI regulations apply until 20 November 2026; watch for the new ESI rules. | Draft the HR budget for 2027-28. Review insurance and benefit renewals. |
| December 2026 | Calendar leave year? Carry forward up to the limit (30 days for OSH Code workers; 45 in Karnataka and Maharashtra) and pay out the rest. The December welfare fund period closes in half-yearly states. | Publish the 2027 holiday list. Finalise the HR budget. Prepare the POSH committee's annual report for 2026. |
| January 2027 | File the Q3 TDS statement by 31 January. Send the POSH committee's 2026 annual report to the employer and the District Officer. Collect proofs from old-regime employees. | Start year-end performance reviews. Hold a POSH awareness refresher for all employees. |
| February 2027 | In Karnataka and Maharashtra, professional tax in the top slab is ₹300 in February instead of ₹200. | Calibrate ratings across teams. Set the increment budget. |
| March 2027 | Make sure each employee's TDS for the full year is deducted by the March payroll. Prepare for the DPDP Act's main duties, which apply from May 2027. | Prepare increment and promotion letters for April. Review policies for the new year. |

### After the year ends

- **30 April 2027:** deposit TDS deducted in March 2027.
- **31 May 2027:** file the Q4 2026-27 TDS statement (Form 138).
- **15 June 2027:** give every employee Form 130 for tax year 2026-27.

## Dates that depend on your state

These differ by state, so add them from your state's labour and tax department websites:

- Professional tax payments and returns
- Labour welfare fund contributions, which are half-yearly (June and December) in most states that have a fund
- Shops and Establishments registration renewals, and annual returns under the Labour Codes and state rules
- The national and festival holidays your state's law requires

<Callout type="tip" title="Keep the proof">
Save every challan, acknowledgement and filed return in one folder per month. If an inspector or auditor asks, you can show each deadline was met in minutes, not days.
</Callout>
