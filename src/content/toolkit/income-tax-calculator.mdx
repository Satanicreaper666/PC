## How the tax is worked out

- **Taxable income** is salary minus the standard deduction (₹75,000 new, ₹50,000 old), plus other income. In the old regime you also subtract the HRA exemption, investments up to ₹1,50,000, health insurance, home-loan interest up to ₹2,00,000 and professional tax.
- **HRA exemption** (old regime only) is the least of the HRA received, rent minus 10% of basic + DA, and 50% of basic + DA in Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad (40% elsewhere).
- **Rebate:** a resident with taxable income up to ₹12 lakh pays no tax under the new regime (up to ₹5 lakh under the old). Just above ₹12 lakh, marginal relief limits the tax to the income above ₹12 lakh.
- **Surcharge** applies above ₹50 lakh, with marginal relief at each threshold. **Cess** of 4% is added to the tax and surcharge.

The example is the TDS handbook's Priya: in Bengaluru, ₹18 lakh salary, ₹9 lakh basic, ₹3.6 lakh HRA, ₹3 lakh rent, ₹1.5 lakh of investments and ₹25,000 of health insurance. Her ₹3.87 lakh of claims leaves the old regime more expensive, so the new regime wins.

<Callout type="note" title="An estimate, not a return">
The calculator covers a resident's salary and simple other income. It leaves out capital gains and other special-rate income, relief for salary arrears, perquisite values and losses. Use it to choose a regime and check TDS; the return is worked out on the year's actual figures.
</Callout>
