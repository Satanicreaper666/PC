## How to use your results

<Figure title="From score to development plan" caption="Work through the steps in order. The comparison in step 3 keeps the plan honest.">
  <Steps
    items={[
      { title: "Answer for how you work now", body: "Rate what you do today. There are no right answers, and nothing is sent anywhere." },
      { title: "Find your two lowest areas", body: "Look at the bars, not just the overall score. Your two lowest areas are your development priorities." },
      { title: "Compare with someone else's view", body: "Ask your manager or a trusted peer to rate you on the same statements, and talk about the gaps." },
      { title: "Set one 90-day goal for each priority", body: "Make it practical, such as building an attrition report or leading one policy change." },
      { title: "Retake it in six months", body: "See which bars have moved, and choose your next priorities." }
    ]}
  />
</Figure>

Two statements are worded the other way round, about going along with wrong decisions and about time spent on administration. The score flips them, so a higher score is always better.
