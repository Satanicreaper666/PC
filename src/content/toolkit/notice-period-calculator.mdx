### Days or months

- **Days:** the notice ends that many calendar days later. 90 days from 2 November 2026 ends on Sunday 31 January 2027.
- **Months:** the notice ends on the same date that many months later. When that date does not exist, it ends on the last day of the shorter month: one month from 31 January 2027 ends on 28 February 2027.
- **When notice starts:** notice is usually counted from the day after it is given. If yours counts the day it is given as day one, every date moves one day earlier.

Weekends and holidays inside the notice period count as notice unless the contract says otherwise. A notice period can end on a Sunday; agree the handover for the working day before.

## Notice is contractual

<Callout type="law" title="What the law says">
No Indian law sets a general notice period for an employee who resigns from a private employer; the appointment letter or contract does. The law sets notice mainly when an employer retrenches a worker: the Industrial Relations Code (section 70) requires <Fact id="notice.retrenchmentNoticeMonths" />'s written notice, or wages in lieu, for a worker with a year's continuous service.

Standing orders also matter in larger establishments: under the Model Standing Orders, 2026, an employer gives a permanent worker one month's notice or wages in lieu, and no notice is due when a fixed-term contract ends or is not renewed. Some state Shops and Establishments Acts require employer notice too.

### Buy-outs, waivers and leave

A buy-out (paying instead of serving notice) and a waiver (the employer letting notice go) bring the last day forward. The calculator moves the date only. How a buy-out is priced, and whether leave can be set off against notice, depend on the contract and policy, so neither is worked out here.
