## How it works

The mail merge uses the same letters as the rest of the toolkit. Each letter has a set of details, such as the candidate's name, the start date and the CTC, and the merge fills them in from one row of your list at a time.

Dates are written the Indian way, so 02/11/2026 becomes 2 November 2026, and amounts in rupees with Indian digit grouping, so 1200000 or 12 LPA becomes ₹12,00,000. Paragraphs, list items and table rows that only hold optional details are left out of a letter when those details are empty, exactly as on each letter's own page. Forms keep them as lines to fill in by hand.

Your own export works too: headings such as Emp Code, Employee Name, Designation, DOJ and Annual CTC are matched for you, and you can change any match before the letters are made.

| The check | What it means | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A required detail is empty | The letter would have a gap, such as no start date | Needs fixing: held back unless you include it |
| A date or amount can't be read | For example 31/02/2026, or "about 5 lakh" | Needs fixing: held back unless you include it |
| A choice isn't one of the letter's options | The text is used as written | Check: included, but read it in the preview |
| An email or number looks wrong | For example "asha@" in an email column | Check: included, but read it in the preview |

## Your data stays on your device

<Callout type="note" title="What happens to your list">
  <ul>
    <li>Your browser reads the file, fills in the letters and makes the Word file on your device. The list is never uploaded to PeopleCraft or anyone else.</li>
    <li>Nothing is stored. Reload or close the page, and the list is gone.</li>
    <li>The Word file and the printout are yours to keep safe. They hold the names, pay and dates of everyone in the list.</li>
  </ul>
</Callout>

Employee and candidate details are personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Its Rules were notified in November 2025, and most of the duties they place on organisations apply after an 18-month phase-in. Working on your own device means the list doesn't pass to another service, but the letters still need the same care as any personnel record: share them only with the people they are for.
