After that, update people when you said you would, even if there is little new to say. Within 30 days, review what happened, what worked and what you would change.

## Legal notices to check

| Situation | What the law requires | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Laying off 'workers' (broadly, non-managerial staff) with a year's service | One month's written notice with reasons, or wages in lieu; compensation of 15 days' average pay per completed year of service; notice to the government; 15 days' wages paid into the worker re-skilling fund. Prior government permission if the establishment is a factory, mine or plantation with 300 or more workers. | Industrial Relations Code, 2020: sections 70 and 83, Chapter X |
| Anyone who resigns or is removed, dismissed or retrenched | Final wages paid within two working days | Code on Wages, 2019: section 17(2) |
| An accident that kills someone or keeps them off work for 48 hours or more, or a listed dangerous occurrence | Notice to the authorities in the form and within the time the rules set | OSH Code, 2020: sections 10 and 11 |
| A cyber incident, including a data breach or unauthorised access | Report to CERT-In within six hours of noticing it, for the incident types listed | CERT-In directions of 28 April 2022 |
| A personal data breach | From May 2027: intimate the Data Protection Board and each affected person | DPDP Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025 |

For managers and others outside the 'worker' definition, notice comes from the employment contract and any state Shops and Establishments Act. If the company is listed, involve the company secretary before announcing layoffs or a senior leader's exit, as stock exchange disclosure rules may apply.

## Layoffs

### Message to all employees

Send this only after everyone affected has been told individually.

<Message label="Layoffs: message to all employees" subject="An important update about our team">

Team,

I want to share difficult news with you directly. Because of [the specific business reason, such as the loss of our largest client], we have decided to reduce our team by [number] roles across [teams or locations].

We did not take this decision lightly. Before reaching it, we [steps already taken, such as pausing hiring and cutting other costs].

**What is happening**

- [Number] roles are affected. Everyone affected has been told individually today.
- Colleagues who are leaving will receive [notice or pay in lieu, compensation, insurance continuation, outplacement support]. Their final settlement will be paid on time.
- If your manager has not spoken to you today, your role is not affected by this decision.

**What happens next**

- Your manager will meet your team by [day] to answer questions.
- HR is available for one-to-one conversations at [place, channel or email].
- If you would like to talk to someone in confidence, [counselling or employee assistance details].

This is a hard day. Our departing colleagues have contributed a great deal, and I ask everyone to treat them with the respect they deserve.

[Name]<br />[Designation]

</Message>

### The one-to-one conversation

- Say it in the first minute: "I have difficult news. Your role is one of those affected by the restructuring we are announcing today."
- Give the reason briefly. If it is about the role and not the person, say so clearly.
- Explain what they will receive and when, and hand it over in writing, including the termination letter.
- Explain what happens to their work, access and belongings, and don't rush them out of the building.
- Tell them who to contact with questions, and when they will hear from HR.
- Listen. Don't argue about the decision or offer opinions about who else was affected.

## A serious workplace incident

### First message to employees

<Message label="Workplace incident: first message to employees" subject="Incident at [site] today">

Team,

At about [time] today, there was [a brief, factual description, such as a fire in the stores area] at [site].

[The people involved are receiving medical care. / Everyone has been accounted for.] Out of respect for the people involved and their families, we will not share names or medical details without their consent.

**What we have done**

- [Made the area safe and called emergency services.]
- [Informed the authorities as the law requires.]
- [Started an investigation, led by name or role.]

**What we ask of you**

- [Avoid the area until it is cleared. / Work from location until further notice.]
- If you saw what happened, please speak to [name], who is collecting information for the investigation.
- If you are affected by what happened, [counselling or employee assistance details].

We will update you by [time and day].

[Name]<br />[Designation]

</Message>

## A personal data breach

### Message to affected employees

<Message label="Personal data breach: message to affected employees" subject="A security incident involving your personal data">

Dear [name],

We are writing to tell you about a security incident that affects your personal data.

**What happened:** On [date], we found [a brief, factual description, such as unauthorised access to a payroll file].

**What data was involved:** [The specific items, such as names, employee IDs, bank account numbers and PAN.]

**What we have done:** [Secured the affected systems, reported the incident to CERT-In, brought in a security firm, and any other steps.]

**What you can do**

- Check your bank accounts and report anything unusual to your bank straight away.
- Be careful with calls, emails or messages that mention this incident or ask for passwords, OTPs or bank details. We will never ask you for these.
- Change the password on any account where you used the same password as your work account.

We are sorry this happened. If you have questions, contact [name] at [email or phone]. We will update you by [date].

[Name]<br />[Designation]

</Message>

## A senior leader leaves

### Announcement to employees

Agree the wording with the leader if you can. Don't speculate about reasons, and never criticise them.

<Message label="Senior leader exit: announcement to employees" subject="Leadership update">

Team,

[Name], our [role], will leave [company] on [date] [to take up a new role elsewhere, or another reason they have agreed to share].

[One or two specific sentences about what they built or changed.]

From [date], [name of the interim or new leader] will lead [function]. Our priorities for [function] stay the same, and current projects continue as planned.

We will hold a session for the [function] team on [date]. If you have questions before then, please speak to [name].

[CEO name]

</Message>

## A critical public review

### Reply on a review site or social media

Reply once, briefly, and never discuss the reviewer's employment details or try to identify an anonymous reviewer.

<Message label="Reply to a critical public review">

Thank you for taking the time to share your experience. We are sorry it fell short, particularly on [the area mentioned].

We have been working on [a specific, true change, such as manager training or clearer promotion criteria], and feedback like yours helps us check whether it is working.

If you are willing to tell us more, please write to [email address].

[Name], People team

</Message>
