faq:
  - q: Who is eligible for statutory bonus?
    a: "An employee whose wages are {{fact:bonus.wageCeiling}} or less and who worked at least 30 days in the accounting year, unless dismissed for fraud, riotous or violent behaviour at work, theft, misappropriation or sabotage of property, or convicted of sexual harassment."
  - q: How is statutory bonus calculated?
    a: "Take each month's wages, or {{fact:bonus.calcCeiling}} (or the minimum wage, if higher) if wages are more, add them up for the months worked, and apply the rate: at least {{fact:bonus.minRate}} and at most {{fact:bonus.maxRate}}. The minimum bonus is ₹100."
  - q: Is statutory bonus paid to someone who leaves mid-year?
    a: Yes, in proportion to the months worked, if they meet the conditions. Many employers pay it with the full and final settlement rather than waiting for the annual payout.
