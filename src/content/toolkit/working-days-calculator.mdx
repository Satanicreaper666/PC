## Using it for leave

To see how many days of leave a holiday will take, enter the first and last day of the trip and your holiday list. The result is the leave you would use if your policy does not count weekly offs and holidays inside the leave. Some policies apply a "sandwich" rule, counting a weekly off or holiday as leave when there is leave on both sides of it; check yours.

This calculator uses no statutory rates. Its dates come from the calendar and from the weekly offs and holidays you enter.
