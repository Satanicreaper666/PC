## How to use your results

Your overall score is the average of all 24 answers, from 1 to 5, shown to one decimal place. The bars show each of the six areas, so you can see where you are strong and where to start.

| Level | Average score | What it usually looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reactive | 1.0 to 1.9 | Firefighting, missed filings, few written processes |
| Compliant | 2.0 to 2.7 | Legal basics in place, processes depend on individuals |
| Operational | 2.8 to 3.4 | Reliable processes, but HR mostly reacts to the business |
| Strategic | 3.5 to 4.2 | Data-informed, linked to business plans |
| Integrated | 4.3 to 5.0 | People decisions are part of how the business is run |

<Callout type="note" title="Not a benchmark">
This assessment shows where your own function stands. It doesn't compare you with other companies, and a high score in one area doesn't make up for legal gaps in another.
</Callout>
