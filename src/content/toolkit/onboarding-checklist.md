## From acceptance to day 1 (pre-boarding)

The weeks between the offer and day 1 are when most offer drop-outs happen. Keep in touch.

- [ ] Send a welcome message within two days of acceptance, from the manager, not only HR (Manager)
- [ ] Keep in touch every week or two until day 1: a call, team news, an invitation to a team lunch (Manager)
- [ ] Answer questions about pay, the role and the joining date quickly, in writing (HR)
- [ ] If the joiner is relocating, help with travel, temporary stay and local information (HR)
- [ ] Watch for warning signs (slow replies, a pushed-back date) and call the joiner if you see them (HR)

## Before day 1

Useful templates: the [employee joining form](/toolkit/employee-joining-form/), the [document checklist](/toolkit/document-checklist/) and the [appointment letter](/toolkit/appointment-letter/).

- [ ] Confirm the start date, time, address and who to ask for, in writing (HR)
- [ ] Send the joining form link and the list of documents to bring (HR)
- [ ] Start background verification, after getting the joiner's written consent (HR)
- [ ] Prepare the appointment letter (HR)
- [ ] Set up the laptop, email and system access so they work on day 1 (IT)
- [ ] Arrange any adjustments the joiner asked for, such as equipment or an accessible workspace (HR)
- [ ] Plan the first week's schedule and a first small task (Manager)
- [ ] Choose a buddy and brief them on their role (Manager)
- [ ] Tell the team who is joining, when, and what they will do (Manager)

## Day 1

- [ ] Welcome the new joiner in person; the manager should be there (Manager)
- [ ] Give the appointment letter and get a signed copy back (HR)
- [ ] Collect the joining form and documents; check originals and hand them back (HR)
- [ ] Start PF and ESI registration and the nomination forms (HR)
- [ ] Explain pay dates, leave, working hours, attendance and who to ask for what (HR)
- [ ] Explain the policy on preventing sexual harassment and how to raise a complaint (HR)
- [ ] Give a safety briefing: emergency exits, first aid and any safety rules for the role (Admin)
- [ ] Hand over the ID card, laptop and access cards, and check that logins work (IT)
- [ ] Introduce the buddy and the team, and have lunch together (Manager)
- [ ] Agree what the first week will cover (Manager)

## Week 1

- [ ] Introduce the joiner to the key people they will work with (Manager)
- [ ] Complete required training: code of conduct, data security and harassment prevention (Employee)
- [ ] Agree written 30-60-90 day goals (Manager and employee)
- [ ] Finish a first small piece of real work (Employee)
- [ ] Hold an end-of-week check-in: what's clear, what's confusing, what's missing (Manager)
- [ ] Confirm the payroll details so the first salary is right (HR)

## Day 30

- [ ] Review progress against the 30-day goals and agree any support needed (Manager)
- [ ] Check in on how onboarding is going and whether anything is missing (HR)
- [ ] Confirm PF, ESI and insurance enrolment is complete and the first payslip is correct (HR)
- [ ] Close background verification; if anything is unclear, talk to the employee before drawing conclusions (HR)

## Day 60

- [ ] Hold a mid-point review against the goals, with specific written feedback (Manager)
- [ ] Agree a learning plan for any skill gaps (Manager)
- [ ] Ask about workload and how well the person feels part of the team (Manager)
- [ ] If there are concerns, raise them now with examples and the support you will give (Manager)

## Day 90

- [ ] Hold a 90-day review against the goals agreed in week 1 (Manager)
- [ ] If probation ends now, confirm employment in writing, or extend it with written reasons, clear goals and a review date (Manager and HR)
- [ ] If probation runs longer, tell the person clearly how they are doing against it (Manager)
- [ ] Set goals for the rest of the year (Manager and employee)
- [ ] Ask for feedback on onboarding and improve this checklist (HR)
