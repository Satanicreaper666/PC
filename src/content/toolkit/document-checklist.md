## Before joining

Templates for this stage: [offer letter](/toolkit/offer-letter/), [interview evaluation form](/toolkit/interview-evaluation-form/), [reference check report](/toolkit/reference-check-report/) and [pre-employment medical declaration](/toolkit/pre-employment-medical-declaration/).

- [ ] Signed copy of the offer letter
- [ ] An interview evaluation form from each interviewer
- [ ] Reference check reports, with the candidate's written agreement to the checks
- [ ] The candidate's written consent to background verification, and the verification report
- [ ] Medical fitness outcome, only if the role needs one; file the declaration itself separately

## Identity and qualifications

Collect these with the [employee joining form](/toolkit/employee-joining-form/).

- [ ] Completed and signed joining form
- [ ] Copy of the PAN card, for tax deduction
- [ ] One proof of identity and address, such as a passport, voter ID, driving licence or masked Aadhaar
- [ ] Copies of the qualifications the role requires; originals checked and returned
- [ ] Professional registration or licence, if the role requires one
- [ ] Relieving letter from the previous employer, or proof the person has resigned there
- [ ] Statement of income and tax deducted by the previous employer in this tax year, if the person changed jobs mid-year
- [ ] Photograph for the ID card

## Statutory forms on joining

The [appointment letter](/toolkit/appointment-letter/) template follows the format in the OSH (Central) Rules, 2026.

- [ ] Appointment letter in the format the OSH Code rules require, signed by both sides, with a copy given to the employee
- [ ] PF joining declaration and the employee's PF nomination, in the EPFO's current formats
- [ ] ESI registration and family details, if the employee is covered by ESI
- [ ] Income-tax declaration for the year, including the employee's choice of tax regime
- [ ] Professional tax: add the employee to your state's records, where the state levies it
- [ ] Health insurance enrolment, with family details if the policy covers family
- [ ] Signed acknowledgement of key policies: code of conduct, prevention of sexual harassment, leave, IT and data security
- [ ] Confidentiality agreement, if it is separate from the appointment letter

## After one year of service

- [ ] Gratuity nomination: ordinarily within 90 days of completing one year (Form III under the Social Security (Central) Rules, 2026; state rules may use their own form)

## During employment

- [ ] Probation review and confirmation letter, or an extension letter with reasons and a review date
- [ ] Annual performance appraisal forms
- [ ] Salary revision, promotion and transfer letters
- [ ] Training records and certificates
- [ ] Warnings, inquiry papers and disciplinary decisions, with the employee's replies
- [ ] Updated bank details and nominations whenever the employee reports a change

## When the employee leaves

- [ ] Resignation letter and its acceptance, or the termination letter
- [ ] Exit interview notes
- [ ] No-dues clearance
- [ ] Full and final settlement statement; wages due must be paid within two working days of the employee leaving (Code on Wages)
- [ ] Gratuity payment record, if eligible; gratuity is due within 30 days of becoming payable
- [ ] Relieving letter and experience letter

## Keeping the file safe

Bank details and health information are sensitive personal data under the IT Act's SPDI Rules. Collect them only when needed, protect them, and don't keep them longer than you need them.

- [ ] Store medical and health records separately from the personnel file
- [ ] Limit access to HR and payroll, and keep paper files locked
- [ ] Set a retention period for each type of document and delete records when it ends. A common starting point, to check with your lawyer: wage, attendance and leave registers and payroll records for at least as long as the Labour Codes and your state's rules require (often three to eight years); tax records for at least eight years after the tax year; PF, ESI and gratuity records while the person is employed and for several years after; candidate records of people you didn't hire for no more than six months, unless they agree to longer
- [ ] Record the retention periods in the [employee privacy notice](/toolkit/employee-privacy-notice/), so employees know them
