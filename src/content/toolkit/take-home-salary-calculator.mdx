The calculator builds the salary structure the way most offer letters do. Basic pay and dearness allowance (DA) are a share of CTC, HRA is a share of basic, and a special allowance takes up whatever is left once the employer's own costs are covered. Because those employer costs depend on wages, and wages include the special allowance, the calculator solves the structure so that every part adds back to the CTC.

## The 50% wage rule

Under the Code on Wages and the Code on Social Security, "wages" are basic pay, DA and retaining allowance, plus any other pay that is not on the exclusion list. A special allowance is not on the list, so it usually counts. HRA, conveyance, overtime, commission and statutory bonus are excluded, but only up to a point: if the excluded items add up to more than <Fact id="wages.halfRule" /> of total pay, the excess is added back to wages.

## What goes into each deduction

- **PF:** 12% from you and 12% from the employer, worked out on wages up to the PF wage ceiling of <Fact id="epf.wageCeiling" /> unless your employer contributes on full wages. Details are on the [EPF calculator](/toolkit/epf-calculator/).
- **ESI:** only if wages are <Fact id="esi.wageCeiling" /> or less, at <Fact id="esi.employeeRate" /> from you and <Fact id="esi.employerRate" /> from the employer. See the [ESI calculator](/toolkit/esi-calculator/).
- **Professional tax:** set by the state where you work, and nil in some states. See the [professional tax calculator](/toolkit/professional-tax-calculator/).
- **Gratuity provision:** many employers include 15 ÷ 26 of a month's wages a year in CTC. It is paid only when you leave after the qualifying service; see the [gratuity calculator](/toolkit/gratuity-calculator/).

<Callout type="warning" title="Income tax is not deducted here">
TDS on salary comes off after these deductions and depends on the tax regime you choose and the declarations you make. The [TDS handbook](/handbooks/tds/salary-tds/) explains how it is worked out under the Income-tax Act, 2025.
</Callout>

Some employers also put the PF admin charge and EDLI insurance cost into CTC, or include variable pay, meal cards and insurance premiums. If your offer letter does, the in-hand figure here will be higher than yours by those amounts.
