## How the amount is worked out

Each completed year of service earns <Fact id="gratuity.daysPerYear" /> of wages. A month's wages are divided by <Fact id="gratuity.divisor" /> to get a day's wages, because the law treats a month as 26 working days. The total cannot exceed <Fact id="gratuity.ceiling" />, the ceiling the Central Government has notified.

The rounding rule only affects the amount. Someone who leaves after 4 years and 11 months has not completed five years, so nothing is payable on resignation, even though the final 11 months would count as a year in the formula.

<Callout type="law" title="What the Code says">
The Code on Social Security, 2020 (section 53) makes gratuity payable after <Fact id="gratuity.minYears" /> of continuous service on superannuation, retirement, resignation, death, disablement or the end of a fixed-term contract. The Industrial Relations Code defines fixed-term employment and makes a fixed-term employee eligible after <Fact id="gratuity.fixedTermMinYears" /> of service under the contract.
</Callout>

### What counts as continuous service

Service stays continuous through breaks for sickness, accident, leave, a lay-off, a strike, a lock-out or a stoppage of work that is not the employee's fault. A year in which the employee was not continuously employed can still count if they actually worked <Fact id="gratuity.continuousServiceDays" /> in the 12 months before the date that matters.

## What counts as wages

Under the Labour Codes, "wages" means basic pay, dearness allowance and retaining allowance, plus any other pay that is not on the exclusion list. A special allowance usually counts. HRA, conveyance, overtime, commission and statutory bonus are excluded, but if those excluded items add up to more than <Fact id="wages.halfRule" /> of total pay, the excess is added back to wages. The [take-home salary calculator](/toolkit/take-home-salary-calculator/) runs that check for a salary structure.

<Callout type="tip">
The Labour Ministry's FAQs say the new definition applies from 21 November 2025. For someone who joined earlier, the amount is still worked out on the last drawn wages under the new definition.
</Callout>

The employer must pay within <Fact id="gratuity.paymentDays" /> of gratuity becoming payable. Late payment carries simple interest. The Code lets an employer forfeit gratuity in limited cases: up to the loss caused, where employment ends for wilful damage to the employer's property, and wholly or partly, where it ends for riotous or violent conduct or an offence involving moral turpitude.

## What this calculator leaves out

- **Near-misses.** The calculator follows the Code's text: five completed years. It does not treat 4 years and 240 days as five years.
- **Seasonal and piece-rated work, and working journalists.** These have their own rules.
- **A fixed-term contract that ends early.** How gratuity applies is not settled.
- **Income tax.** Gratuity up to a limit can be tax-free, but the Income-tax Act, 2025 provision still needs confirming. See the TDS handbook chapter on [joiners and leavers](/handbooks/tds/joiners-leavers-and-transfers/) and ask a tax adviser.

Contracts, awards and company policies can pay more than the law requires. They cannot pay less.
