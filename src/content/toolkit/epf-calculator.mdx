The employee contributes <Fact id="epf.employeeRate" /> of PF wages and the employer matches it. For a member of the Employees' Pension Scheme (EPS), <Fact id="epf.epsRate" /> of wages up to the ceiling goes from the employer's share to pension; the rest goes to the PF account. The employer also pays EDLI insurance at <Fact id="epf.edliRate" /> and an admin charge at <Fact id="epf.adminRate" />, outside the 12%.

## What PF wages are

PF is worked out on "wages" as the Code on Social Security defines them: basic pay, dearness allowance and retaining allowance, plus any other pay that is not on the exclusion list. If HRA, conveyance and other excluded items add up to more than <Fact id="wages.halfRule" /> of total pay, the excess counts as wages too. The [take-home salary calculator](/toolkit/take-home-salary-calculator/) runs that check.

PF applies to establishments with <Fact id="epf.coverageEmployees" /> or more. September 2026 was a split month: 1 to 16 September used the ₹15,000 ceiling and 17 to 30 September used ₹25,000, in a single return due by 15 October 2026.

<Callout type="note" title="Lower rate for a few industries">
A rate of <Fact id="epf.reducedRate" /> instead of 12% applies to establishments under an insolvency resolution or repayment plan, and to jute, beedi, brick, coir (other than spinning) and guar-gum units. This calculator uses 12%.
</Callout>

## What this calculator leaves out

- Interest, which EPFO credits once a year.
- Whether someone qualifies for EPS, which depends on joining dates and past options. Enter the answer instead.
- International workers, who have no wage ceiling.
- The minimum admin charge of <Fact id="epf.adminMinimum" />, which applies to the establishment as a whole, not to each employee.
