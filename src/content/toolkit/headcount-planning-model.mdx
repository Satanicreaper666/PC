## How the plan is built

The plan starts from today's headcount, adds new roles across the year and replaces everyone expected to leave, so the year ends where you planned.

<Figure title="Hires for the year" caption="Expected leavers are worked out month by month from the planned headcount, so they rise as the team grows.">
  <Formula
    result={{ value: "Hires for the year" }}
    parts={[{ value: "New roles", label: "net growth you plan" }, { op: "+" }, { value: "Expected leavers", label: "headcount × attrition % ÷ 12, each month" }]}
    example="Example: 40 new roles + 39 expected leavers = 79 hires. Headcount goes from 200 to 240, and averages 220 over the year."
  />
</Figure>

Payroll cost follows the headcount month by month: average headcount in the month × average CTC ÷ 12, plus any employer costs outside CTC. In the example, 220 people on average × ₹12,00,000 = ₹26.4 crore for the year.

Replacements are different: you can only start when someone resigns. If hiring takes longer than the notice period, every replacement leaves a seat empty for a while.

## Agree the inputs first

| Input | Where it comes from | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attrition rate | The last 12 months, from the [HR metrics calculator](/toolkit/hr-metrics-calculator/) | Known changes, such as a new office or a restructure |
| New roles | Business heads' plans for the year | Roles that depend on revenue targets being met |
| Days to hire | Your applicant tracking system | Senior and niche roles take much longer |
| Average CTC | Payroll | New hires can cost more than the current average |

The plan feeds the hiring line of the [HR budget builder](/toolkit/hr-budget-builder/), and the [attrition cost calculator](/toolkit/attrition-cost-calculator/) puts a rupee figure on the leavers.

<Callout type="note" title="What the model assumes">
  Attrition is steady through the year, every leaver is replaced, and new joiners cost the current average CTC. Monthly figures are whole people, rounded from running totals so the months always add up to the year.
</Callout>
