## How it is worked out

For each person on the rolls on the date (people with a last working day are left out):

1. **Service** runs from the date of joining to the chosen date. A part-year of more than six months counts as a full year for the amount.
2. **Accrued gratuity** is monthly wages × <Fact id="gratuity.daysPerYear" short /> ÷ <Fact id="gratuity.divisor" /> × years counted, capped at <Fact id="gratuity.ceiling" short />.
3. **Vested** if the person has completed <Fact id="gratuity.minYears" /> of service, or <Fact id="gratuity.fixedTermMinYears" /> for fixed-term employees. The rest is accrued but would be paid only on death or disablement, or once they reach the minimum.

The [gratuity calculator](/toolkit/gratuity-calculator/) works out one person's gratuity in detail, with forfeiture and tax.
