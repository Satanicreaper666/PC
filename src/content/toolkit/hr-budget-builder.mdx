faq:
  - q: What should an HR budget include?
    a: The HR team's own cost, hiring, learning and development, HR software, engagement and recognition, wellbeing programmes, compliance and legal costs, and a contingency. Other employees' salaries and statutory contributions usually sit in the payroll budget instead.
  - q: What percentage of revenue should HR spend?
    a: There is no reliable single figure for India. It varies with industry, size and how much HR work is outsourced. Track your own figure year on year and explain changes by what the business needs.
  - q: How do I estimate the hiring budget?
    a: Add new roles to expected replacements (headcount × attrition rate), then multiply by your cost per hire. The cost per hire calculator helps you find that figure.
  - q: Is employer PF part of the HR budget?
    a: Usually not. Employer PF, ESI and gratuity are part of each employee's CTC and sit in the payroll budget. Keeping them out of the HR budget avoids counting them twice.

## What belongs in the HR budget

| Line | Include | Usually not here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HR team | CTC of HR staff, shared roles as fractions | Payroll of other teams |
| Hiring | Job boards, agencies, assessments, checks, recruiters' time | Joining salaries |
| Learning and development | Programmes, trainers, platforms, certifications | Pay of people while they attend |
| HR software | HR system, payroll and attendance software | Laptops and IT for everyone |
| Engagement and wellbeing | Recognition, surveys, events, wellbeing programmes | Group health insurance premiums, which are often part of CTC |
| Compliance and legal | Audits, legal retainers, registrations and renewals | Statutory contributions such as PF and ESI |

## Presenting it to finance

1. Lead with the total, the per-employee figure and the change from last year.
2. Tie each line to a business outcome: hiring to the [headcount plan](/toolkit/headcount-planning-model/), learning to skills the business needs, engagement to [what attrition costs](/toolkit/attrition-cost-calculator/).
3. Say what you would cut first if the budget is reduced, and what that would cost the business.

<Callout type="tip">
  Agree the contingency rule with finance in advance, such as "released only with CFO approval". It keeps the budget honest without hiding money in every line.
</Callout>
