    a: Yes. People answer more honestly when they can't be identified. Report results only for groups of five or more, and never show a manager individual answers or comments that could identify someone.

<Callout type="warning" title="Protect anonymity">
Never show a manager individual answers, or comments that could identify someone. If fewer than five people answer, combine the results with another team or wait for the next round.
</Callout>

## Reading the scores

- Each area score is the average of its three statements, on a scale of 1 to 5.
- Start with any area below 3. That is where the team most needs something to change.
- Two statements are worded the other way round, about worrying over disagreeing and the manager being hard to reach. The score flips them, so a higher score is always better.
- Compare a manager's scores with their own earlier results, not with other managers' teams, which differ in work and size.
